Five palettes

Same board, five colour systems. Each passes WCAG AA for text. Click a palette to open the board in it.

1 · Washi and sumi

Paper and ink, with deep moss for the one field. Calm, crafted, Japanese restraint.

2 · Iron and ash

Cool ash ground, iron blue-black ink and field. Precise, discreet, the colder of the five.

#EDEFEE#DFE3E2#152120#53605E#1F3634

3 · Stone and kakishibu

Warm stone with persimmon-tannin brown, the colour of the stairwell and the lit window.

#ECE9E3#DFDAD1#231C17#5E554D#6F4428

4 · Night window

The one dark option: night blue-black with the lamp-light of the lit window as its only accent.

#12161B#1C222A#ECE8E1#A4A8AE#E3A35A

5 · Porcelain and oxblood

Near-white porcelain and one deep oxblood field. The most formal: private bank, fine leather.

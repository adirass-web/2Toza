Toza: three directions

Concept boards for Dr. Lior Tabansky. Each shows the same content: the home page's first screen and the situations, at desktop and phone width, with its own wordmark. The words are unchanged from the approved site; only the design differs. Open each board on a phone too.

2 · The quiet instrument chosen, version 2

Open board 2
Feeling
A private letter: almost nothing on the page, all of it exact
Type
Bona Nova (Latin and Hebrew)
Colour
White, near-black, one mint field
Wordmark
Italic toza with 閉ざす as a colophon (approved)

Idea. Tozasu, to close shut, becomes the page's restraint: a page that shows almost nothing, the way a closed door shows nothing.

What is the same on all three

  • Every word comes from the approved site (2toza main @ b3efb35). A check fails the build if a single new word appears; wordmark notes and photo credits are the only exceptions.
  • WhatsApp, Signal and Call me back open the private channel on the first tap.
  • Each typeface sets Hebrew and English, so the Hebrew site keeps the same look.
  • Motion is held to one quiet moment at most, so the page behaves as discreetly as it speaks.

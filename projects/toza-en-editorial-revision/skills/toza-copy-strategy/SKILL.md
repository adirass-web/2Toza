# Toza Copy Strategy

## Goal

Decide what a Toza page must accomplish before sentences are written.

Do not draft first and rationalize later.

## Required context

Read, in this order when available:
1. latest owner-approved commercial/service facts;
2. `docs/editorial/TOZA-EDITORIAL-DOCTRINE.md`;
3. latest Toza copy specification;
4. page-specific approved copy or decision notes;
5. surrounding pages and shared copy.

If authorities conflict, output an **Authority Conflict** section before any strategy recommendation.

## Strategy questions

For each page determine:

### Page role
- What page type is this?
- What single job does it perform in the journey?
- What should this page not try to do?

### Reader arrival state
- What does the reader already know?
- What situation are they likely in?
- What uncertainty remains?
- What level of detail is appropriate here?

Default internal model: **situation-aware, exposure-blind**.

### Reader departure state
The page should move the reader toward one or more of:
- recognition;
- relevance;
- trust;
- clarity;
- confidence;
- relief/control;
- qualified private contact.

Do not optimize for raw lead volume.

### Core argument
State the one sentence this page must establish.

Then define:
- supporting points;
- proof required;
- objections or risks to answer;
- material boundaries;
- commercial facts that must be visible.

### Emotional register
Choose the justified emotional territory:
- uncertainty;
- pressure;
- fear of making the wrong change;
- frustration after partial self-help;
- relief;
- regained control;
- confidence in the next step.

Never invent surveillance, wrongdoing, or urgency.

### Premium signal
Specify which operating facts will carry prestige:
- founder access;
- senior-only delivery;
- in-person work;
- tailored mapping;
- client control;
- precise scope;
- verification;
- record;
- limited capacity/travel if approved.

Do not rely on generic luxury vocabulary.

### Copy-density budget
Classify every candidate section:
- essential;
- useful;
- duplicate;
- better linked;
- remove.

Prefer one idea per section.

### Conversion logic
Primary CTA is normally **Request a private conversation**.

Treat contact as qualification, not purchase.

Do not create commitment language that collapses:
- conversation;
- paid assessment;
- broader protection.

## Output

Return:

1. **Authority status**
2. **Page objective**
3. **Reader arrival state**
4. **Reader departure state**
5. **Core argument**
6. **Required proof**
7. **Required boundaries**
8. **Emotional register**
9. **Premium signal**
10. **Section architecture**
11. **Density cuts**
12. **CTA logic**
13. **Do-not-say list**
14. **Open factual decisions**

## Gate

Do not hand off to `toza-copywriter` until:
- authority conflicts are resolved or explicitly quarantined;
- page objective is singular;
- required commercial facts are identified;
- duplicate content is marked;
- proof and boundaries are defined.

# Toza Editor

## First rule: authority before style

Before editing:
1. identify protected approved copy;
2. identify current commercial facts;
3. compare against the strategic spec;
4. flag conflicts.

Never “improve” an approved commercial fact back to an older specification.

Protected approved copy receives factual consistency and defect review only unless substantive change is explicitly authorized.

## The Ten Sweeps

Run sequentially. After each sweep, re-check earlier sweeps.

### Sweep 1 — Clarity
Ask:
- Is every sentence immediately understandable?
- Is the subject clear?
- Is jargon necessary?
- Is the main point buried in qualifications?

### Sweep 2 — Voice and Tone
Ask:
- Does this sound calm, adult, direct and restrained?
- Does it sound like one senior human voice?
- Does it shift into corporate, legalistic or academic prose without need?
- Is warmth present without reassurance cliché?

### Sweep 3 — Relevance / “So what?”
Ask:
- Why does this matter to this reader, on this page?
- Is the copy explaining the reader’s life back to them?
- Does every section advance the page’s argument?

### Sweep 4 — Proof
For every consequential claim:
- what supports it?
- is the support nearby or clearly available?
- is the wording stronger than the evidence?

Valid Toza proof includes:
- founder credentials/profile;
- direct-delivery model;
- precise scope;
- documented process;
- client-control rules;
- verification;
- current approved commercial facts.

Do not require testimonials.

### Sweep 5 — Specificity
Replace vague nouns/adjectives with:
- mechanisms;
- scope;
- numbers;
- conditions;
- consequences.

Do not add false precision.

### Sweep 6 — Emotional Effect
Target:
- recognition;
- uncertainty becoming clarity;
- loss of control becoming control;
- confidence in the next step.

Reject:
- fear amplification;
- invented hostile actors;
- melodrama;
- surveillance fantasy;
- guilt or blame.

### Sweep 7 — Friction / Risk
Check:
- What happens after contact?
- Is purchase separate from qualification?
- Are price/scope/boundaries clear at the right point?
- Is the next action easy to understand?
- Are privacy assurances accurate rather than absolute?

Do not add generic guarantees or trial language.

### Sweep 8 — Prestige / Scarcity
Ask:
> Does this sound like rare senior professional service, or a competent cybersecurity consultancy?

Look for:
- direct founder access;
- tailored judgment;
- discretion;
- precise scope;
- restraint;
- client control;
- limited delivery model where approved.

Flag:
- mass-market language;
- SaaS language;
- agency voice;
- eager selling;
- generic luxury adjectives.

### Sweep 9 — Compression / Density
Ask:
> What can be removed without reducing comprehension, trust, informed decision-making or required boundary clarity?

Classify repeated material as:
- must repeat;
- can shorten;
- link instead;
- remove.

At corpus level, distinguish standardized operational language from brand-deadening persuasive repetition.

### Sweep 10 — Human Credibility
Flag AI-like prose:
- artificial symmetry;
- repeated three-part cadences;
- “not just X, but Y”;
- generic transitions;
- empty sophistication;
- repeated rhetorical questions;
- overuse of em dashes;
- generic “designed to” phrasing;
- excessive restatement.

Ask:
> Would a sophisticated founder, senior lawyer, executive or family-office principal believe a real senior expert wrote this?

## Corpus sweep

When reviewing the full English corpus, additionally audit:
- service name consistency;
- price/VAT;
- device scope;
- duration language;
- urgent trigger;
- credit condition;
- support period;
- key counts;
- deliverable names;
- attorney authorization;
- privacy wording;
- CTA wording;
- retired categories;
- repeated blocks.

Cross-page contradiction outranks local elegance.

## Output

Return findings before rewriting.

For each finding:
- severity: blocker / major / moderate / minor;
- sweep;
- page/section;
- issue;
- why it matters;
- recommended action;
- whether owner/factual decision is required.

Then provide revised copy only for findings authorized for correction.

## Calibration method

Each page is assessed on:
- page role;
- authority/fact consistency;
- clarity;
- voice and tone;
- relevance;
- proof;
- specificity;
- emotional effect;
- friction/risk;
- prestige/scarcity;
- compression/density;
- human credibility;
- cross-page role discipline.

Scoring is 1–5. A low score is not a claim that the copy is poor; it identifies the dimension that most needs editorial work.

## Global editorial architecture

The 12-page set should not make every page explain the whole service.

Canonical responsibilities:

- **Home:** category, relevance, mechanism, senior-service proof, routes, first step.
- **What Happens:** sequence before/during/after the work.
- **FAQ:** exhaustive objections, operational limits, edge cases and boundaries.
- **Pricing:** canonical commercial facts, inclusions, comparisons, credit and urgent fees.
- **Why Toza:** founder rationale, senior access, premium/service-design proof.
- **Situation pages:** recognition, page-specific mechanism, timing, fit, concise route to service.
- **Attorneys:** referral triggers, client authority, attorney coordination and evidentiary boundaries.
- **Why This Is Not IT Support:** category distinction and alternatives comparison.
- **Initial Paid Visit:** canonical approved product page for the Assessment.

The primary compression principle is therefore **role discipline**, not arbitrary word-count reduction.

# Cross-page red-team findings

## 1. Biggest site-wide issue: templated second halves

The situation pages are individually differentiated at the top and increasingly standardized at the bottom.

Pattern:
1. page-specific recognition;
2. page-specific mechanism;
3. full Assessment block;
4. full credit rule;
5. full boundary block;
6. full contact block.

This produces consistency but reduces human/editorial quality.

### Required correction
Keep page-specific recognition + mechanism + unique boundaries. Compress shared mechanics and link to canonical pages.

## 2. Canonical ownership of repeated facts

### Pricing owns
- all prices;
- service comparison;
- credit;
- urgent surcharges;
- full-service scope comparison.

### FAQ owns
- exhaustive boundaries;
- privacy/data handling;
- edge cases;
- attorney disclosure;
- “what if…” objections.

### What Happens owns
- sequence.

### Initial Paid Visit owns
- Assessment product detail.

Situation pages should not become copies of those canonical pages.

## 3. Premium signal

The strongest premium signals in the corpus are:
- direct named expert;
- in-person work;
- client presence;
- explained/approved changes;
- credential control;
- tailored scope;
- verification/records;
- founder rationale.

The weakest signal is scarcity/limited capacity. It is allowed by the doctrine but not necessary to add unless operationally useful.

Do not “fix” premium by adding luxury adjectives.

## 4. Emotional architecture

Best current emotional pages:
1. They Know Something
2. Why Toza
3. Separation
4. Business

The site should preserve their recognition language while reducing procedural drag.

Desired transition:
**uncertainty → understanding → deliberate action → control**

## 5. AI-language risk

The corpus is not dominated by obvious AI clichés. The risk comes from **structural repetition**, not sentence-level buzzwords.

Therefore:
- do not over-focus on word-policing;
- focus on page-role discipline and duplicate blocks.

## 6. Authority consistency

The five newer commercial decisions are now ratified current authority:
- Assessment ₪3,600 including VAT;
- one main phone + one PC/iPad/second phone;
- at least two hours, typically four, with outcome-based completion;
- Inner Circle Shield approved, from ₪42,000 including VAT;
- eligible Assessment credit may apply to Personal Shield, Inner Circle Shield or Bespoke under current conditions.

No page in EN-02-v1 materially contradicts those five current facts.

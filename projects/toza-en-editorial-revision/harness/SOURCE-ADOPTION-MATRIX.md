# Toza Editorial Harness — Source Adoption Matrix

Status: draft harness source record  
Branch: `editorial/toza-copy-harness`

## Authority order

The harness does not treat external copywriting frameworks as authority over Toza.

1. Later explicit owner decisions and approved copy.
2. Current Toza commercial/service facts.
3. Toza strategic/editorial specification.
4. Page-specific approved intent and structure.
5. External frameworks and skills.
6. Generic copywriting convention.

When two higher-order sources conflict, flag an **authority conflict**. Do not silently choose.

## Sources reviewed

### Corey Haines / marketingskills — copywriting
Adopt:
- clarity over cleverness;
- specificity over vagueness;
- customer language over company language;
- one idea per section;
- simple, active, direct prose;
- honesty over sensationalism;
- separate drafting from editing.

Adapt:
- “benefits over features” becomes **consequence and relevance over generic benefit language**;
- CTA guidance becomes **qualified private contact**, not maximum-click persuasion;
- page structures are optional diagnostic aids, not templates.

Reject for Toza:
- default social-proof/testimonial architecture;
- formulaic headline patterns as a governing system;
- rhetorical-question and humor defaults;
- aggressive CTA optimization;
- mass-market conversion assumptions.

### Corey Haines / marketingskills — copy-editing / Seven Sweeps
Adopt:
- sequential focused passes;
- Clarity;
- Voice and Tone;
- So What / relevance;
- Prove It;
- Specificity;
- Heightened Emotion;
- Zero Risk / next-step clarity;
- loop back after later edits to ensure earlier gains remain.

Adapt:
- Prove It allows privacy-compatible proof: founder credentials, operating model, precise scope, documented process, client-control rules;
- Heightened Emotion targets recognition, relief, control, and reduced uncertainty, never fear amplification;
- Zero Risk means clear scope, boundaries, process, pricing and next step; guarantees and free-trial tactics are not default reducers.

Add:
- Prestige / Scarcity;
- Compression / Density;
- Human Credibility / AI-trope detection;
- Cross-page consistency;
- Authority-conflict detection.

Reject:
- assumption that testimonials, case studies, review scores or guarantees are required;
- generic “risk reversal” as a premium-service requirement.

### Corey Haines / marketingskills — page CRO
Adopt:
- value-proposition clarity;
- CTA hierarchy;
- scannability;
- objection handling;
- friction analysis;
- page-specific purpose.

Adapt:
- conversion goal is **qualified comprehension → trust → self-identification → private contact**;
- more leads are not intrinsically better;
- premium restraint may justify fewer CTAs and less repetition;
- trust comes primarily from senior access, specificity, boundaries and operating model.

Reject:
- recommendation badges or plan steering as defaults;
- generic customer-logo/testimonial expectations;
- “conversion rate” as the sole success measure.

### RMBC / direct-response methodology
Adopt selectively:
- research before copy;
- mechanism-driven differentiation;
- briefing before drafting;
- audit against claims and mechanism.

Adapt:
- Toza’s mechanism is residual/ordinary access and connected digital exposure, not a dramatic “unique mechanism” sales device.

Reject:
- pain amplification;
- aggressive hooks;
- urgency manufacturing;
- offer stacking;
- direct-response theatrics inconsistent with discreet HNW professional service.

### AI-trope / broad-framework libraries
Adopt selectively:
- detect formulaic AI prose;
- detect generic luxury and cybersecurity language;
- detect repetitive rhetorical symmetry and templated transitions.

Reject:
- “50+ framework” selection as a writing strategy;
- formula proliferation;
- style novelty for its own sake.

## Toza-specific additions

External sources do not adequately cover:
- HNW/private-client prestige through service design;
- discretion as an operating fact rather than a luxury adjective;
- absence of conventional testimonials for privacy reasons;
- founder access as part of the product;
- distinction between qualification, paid assessment and full hardening;
- authority precedence when approved commercial facts supersede older specs;
- cross-page repetition control across a multi-page professional-services site;
- bilingual semantic discipline for later EN→HE adaptation.

These are first-class rules in the Toza harness.

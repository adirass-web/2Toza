## 1. Governing principle

The revision harness must be **strict internally and lightweight for owner review**.

The objective is not maximal rewriting. It is controlled editorial improvement of the 11 unapproved English pages while keeping the approved Initial Paid Visit / PA-01-v2 protected.

The revision system must:
- prevent factual drift;
- reduce cross-page duplication;
- preserve page-specific meaning;
- strengthen premium/private-client signal through operating facts;
- maintain clear purchase, scope, privacy and authority boundaries;
- keep owner review compact and exact.

## 7. Repetition policy

Do not manage repetition by raw word counts alone.

Evaluate **semantic repetition**.

Allowed repetition:
- prices and scope where a decision requires them;
- essential legal/service boundaries;
- credential-control reassurance;
- CTA wording;
- urgent trigger language where relevant.

Flag repetition when:
- two sections perform the same rhetorical job;
- a situation page repeats canonical Pricing/FAQ/Process content without adding page-specific meaning;
- a shared footer repeats an already-complete closing CTA;
- the same reassurance appears several times on one page;
- wording changes but the message does not.

## 8. Minimum trust payload

Compression must not remove the information a reasonable private client needs.

Every relevant page should make clear, at the appropriate level:
- what happens after contact;
- whether contact commits the client;
- who performs the work;
- who controls credentials;
- whether changes require approval;
- what is in scope at a high level;
- where broader work begins;
- where detailed pricing/boundaries can be found.

## 9. Deletion safety rules

Never cut or obscure:
- current price/VAT where commercially required;
- material scope limits;
- purchase boundary;
- client authorization requirements;
- urgent-arrival trigger;
- material legal/non-forensic boundary where context requires it;
- outcome/completion conditions needed to prevent misunderstanding;
- current credit conditions where the page invites a purchase decision.

Premium minimalism must not become under-disclosure.

## 11. Cross-page uniqueness test

Apply primarily to:
- Home;
- Separation and Divorce;
- Business and Founder Dispute;
- They Know Something;
- Inheritance Conflict;
- Why Toza where relevant.

## 12. Metadata rule

Every page revision includes a separate metadata sweep after body copy stabilizes.

Review:
- title;
- meta description;
- social description;
- route-facing labels where relevant.

Metadata is part of exact owner approval.

## 13. Fact-change quarantine

Any proposed change affecting the following is a **Class A factual/commercial change** and must be flagged separately:

- price;
- VAT;
- device scope;
- duration;
- support;
- keys;
- credit;
- urgent conditions;
- deliverables;
- product architecture;
- included network;
- included days;
- eligibility rules.

Never bury a factual change inside prose editing.

## 14. Owner review format

Internal work may be detailed. Owner review must be compact.

For each page present only:

1. **What changed** — maximum 5 bullets.
2. **Exact revised copy** — complete page.
3. **Only unresolved red-team issues**.
4. **Approval state**.

Do not dump every sweep unless it found something material.

Approval states:
- **READY FOR OWNER REVIEW**
- **NEEDS FACT DECISION**
- **APPROVED**
- **PROTECTED / NO CHANGE**

## 15. Revision provenance

Maintain a concise record for each page:
- source version;
- structure rating;
- copy rating;
- sections cut/moved/linked;
- facts preserved;
- owner decisions created;
- protected language preserved;
- metadata status.

Do not log ordinary punctuation or trivial line edits.

# Toza — approved logo and designer brief

Owner approval: 9 October 2026, following review of the fitted-panel logo: “OK give it to the repo and brief our designers to use it.” Use this identity in the current website implementation and subsequent design work. This supersedes all earlier curved-frame, bottom-line and typeset-only logo studies.

## Approved construction

Two fitted solid panels meet at a narrow stepped joint, beside lowercase `toza` in Source Serif 4. The name derives from Japanese 閉ざす (tozasu), “to close shut.” The panels interpret a completed closure and precise fitting. This meaning is design context, not a promise of eliminating every exposure. Pronunciation: TOH-zah.

Use the supplied vector artwork unchanged. Its typography is outlined, optically spaced Source Serif 4; it needs no webfont and is not wholly custom lettering. Preserve the mark-to-name proportions and the central joint. Do not recreate the wordmark as live text, stretch it, round or simplify its seam, add a bottom line, put it back in an ornamental frame, or substitute locks/shields/digital imagery.

## Language and accessibility

The logo always stays Latin lowercase `toza`, including Hebrew layouts. Never mirror, transliterate, uppercase or add Japanese letters. Use `Toza` in prose. For a linked homepage logo, provide a single accessible name such as `Toza home`; avoid repeating it on both link and image. Decorative repeated emblems should have empty alternative text. Preserve native keyboard focus on linked logos.

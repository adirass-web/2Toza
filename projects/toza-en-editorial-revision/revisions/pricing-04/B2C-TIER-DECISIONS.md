## Additional primary example: Bitwarden

[Official personal pricing](https://bitwarden.com/pricing/) was searched and read in full as extracted Markdown. This root review did not inspect a rendered Bitwarden page; visual execution will be evaluated from the designer's evidence.

The extracted personal section leads with two paid choices, Premium and Families. Families includes six premium accounts. The main page separately lists core features shared by every account and supplies a longer individual comparison. Paid cards name benefits and purchase/trial actions. The recurring payment period, annual charge and tax treatment are disclosed. Free account creation is a secondary entry option.

Borrow the separation between shared capability and different quantities: Toza's one-person and four-person engagements have the same individual standard. Group coverage is not a superior grade of personal protection. Show the actual difference in people, device pairs, supplied keys and shared network rather than filling the page with duplicate checkmarks.

Adapt the short paid-choice overview plus a complete comparison. Keep the full one-off engagement price primary. A paid Assessment is a sequential first stage, not a cheaper tier beside Personal Shield or a free trial. Display its fee and outcome on the route to protection, and explain the conditional credit near the remaining balance.

Do not copy the monthly equivalent or account-creation CTA. Toza sells in-person work after a conversation and accepted written scope. “Start free trial” cannot be translated into a free technical consultation. Supported hardware-key capacity on a software plan is not supplied hardware; Toza's two/eight physical keys must remain explicitly supplied, configured and tested.

## Decisions to reconcile after expert research

1. Differentiate by who is protected and scope, rather than good/better/best security.
2. Show short, counted decision attributes first; retain all detailed specifications through labelled access.
3. Present shared threat-led hardening, practical OpSec teaching, testing and records once.
4. Show the first paid Assessment as the means to choose the appropriate full work, with an independently useful practical summary.
5. Keep a single real contact action, full prices and payment conditions visible. Avoid subscription framing, unsupported popularity badges, checkout controls or manufactured urgency.
6. Use compact grouping and readable labels. Graphics must lower the work required to compare; additional illustration height is not itself progress.

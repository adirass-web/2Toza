## Complete scope and support {#full-specification}

### Personal and group scope

Personal Shield covers one person; Inner Circle covers four. Each person's allowance is one main phone and one PC, iPad or second phone. Relevant identities, accounts, apps, permissions, recovery routes and data access are included, including access to messages, photos, documents and location information.

The initial paid Assessment reviews the initial client's two devices and relevant accounts. Review and mapping for the remaining group are included in the separately purchased Inner Circle engagement. Each person receives the same standard of hardening, essential migration where needed, compatible supplied/configured/tested keys, personal teaching and verification.

### Time, records and adjustment support

A typical Personal Shield engagement requires 7–14 hours of in-person work, excluding the initial Assessment. The price includes one primary working day and a second if needed. Inner Circle typically requires 2–4 working days.

Personal Shield includes your private dated record. Inner Circle includes a private dated record per person and a separate shared-network record. Personal details are shared only with that person's consent; group membership does not give participants access to one another's information.

Personal Shield includes 14 days of limited adjustment support for issues directly related to Toza's changes. Inner Circle includes 14 days for all four people and the included network, starting when the group engagement is complete, for issues directly related to Toza's changes. Support is through WhatsApp, Signal and scheduled calls; it does not involve remote control of your devices.

### Network, migration and completion limits

Inner Circle includes one shared home or home-office network and router in addition to the eight personal devices. We review and harden accessible settings and authorized administration. Replacement hardware, additional connected devices and additional locations are quoted separately. Network work for Personal Shield is separately scoped.

Essential migration covers identity, recovery and critical-service access. Historical archives and bulk data migration require a separately agreed scope. A technically blocked or declined action is not presented as completed.

## Service boundaries {#service-details}

Toza works within your authorized digital environment and agreed scope, using what is disclosed, observable or otherwise lawfully accessible. The service does not promise to discover every account or find and remove every exposure. An access path does not establish wrongdoing or past use; changing access cannot recall independent copies already held elsewhere.

Toza does not investigate another person, reconstruct events forensically, collect legal evidence, give legal advice or provide routine IT support. The dated record documents work; it is not a forensic report, evidence document, certification or security guarantee.

You remain present, control your credentials and approve material changes. Toza does not keep credentials for ongoing use, remotely control or monitor devices, or install security software. Your situation is handled confidentially. [How your information is handled](/en/faq.html#privacy) · [Detailed service boundaries](/en/faq.html#boundaries)

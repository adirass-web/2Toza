## Business decisions for Toza

1. **Organise the choice around coverage.** Personal Shield is the principal one-person offer. Inner Circle extends the same individual standard to four and adds the agreed shared network. This is closest to 1Password's individual/group segmentation, with explicit Toza privacy and consent boundaries. No higher-security implication or “best value” claim is justified.

2. **Keep the Assessment in the journey, not beside full services as a cheap tier.** Software can sell instant product access through trial/signup; Toza must first qualify fit/urgency, then deliver paid assessment and later separately accepted implementation. The Assessment's own value is a personal threat model, specific risk assessment and ordered next steps in the existing practical summary, even without further purchase. It must not appear to be a free consultation, merely a deposit or only a sales diagnosis.

3. **Anchor with the full implementation fee.** Show ₪14,000 / from ₪42,000 as full-service prices, and ₪3,600 as the separate initial Assessment payment. Show ₪10,400 / from ₪38,400 only as *remaining payments with eligible credit*. Unlike monthly-equivalent subscription pricing, these amounts describe actual distinct purchases. Keep commencement within 14 days of initial completion, not merely booking, once-per-group and additions beside the comparison. A conditional payment ledger can clarify the relationship; an isolated lower amount would mislead.

4. **Use a short comparison for meaningful differences; show shared work once.** The main comparison should answer people, allowed client devices, supplied compatible keys, included group network, expected work time and payment. The shared treatment explains tailored hardening/essential migration where needed, OpSec practice, testing and private records. Full scope retains support periods, primary/second-day inclusion, initial-client/group mapping, consent and limitations. This borrows quick-choice/full-reference logic without losing canonical commercial disclosure.

5. **Show the value of the work, not only the inventory.** Apple translates storage into a use case; Adobe translates apps into a job. Toza should connect tangible objects and method to the client result: a review built around personal circumstances; risks and ordered priorities; then an implemented setup with configured keys, tested changes and practice using it. A neutral access-model illustration demonstrates the method, not a fictional finding or new formal report.

6. **Keep the action appropriate to professional service.** The CTA remains “Request a private conversation” through neutral WhatsApp/Signal contact. No “Buy now,” checkout, free trial, technical lead magnet or calendar is warranted. Show the next action and value at each stage, while preserving written offer, explicit acceptance/payment and voluntary second purchase. Conversion means a qualified enquiry followed by paid work when suitable, not simply an easy click.

7. **Borrow clarity around conditions, not their discount mechanics.** The examples visibly attach billing/trial/promotion terms to consumer prices. Toza should attach its VAT, fixed/from scope, separate Assessment and credit terms just as carefully. Do not import subscription renewal, trial cancellation, “save X%,” monthly instalments or guarantees absent from Toza authority.

## What this validates, and what remains to test

The research supplies concrete patterns suitable for a proposal, not proof of conversion uplift. Test whether a qualified prospect can identify the first action/fee, the Assessment outcome, full-service versus conditional remaining payment, one/four-person scope, device/key/time counts, the threat-model and OpSec value, and the voluntary second decision. Then measure qualified enquiries, paid Assessments and separately accepted full engagements against a documented baseline. Preserve accurate terms even if a superficially simpler display wins more unqualified clicks.

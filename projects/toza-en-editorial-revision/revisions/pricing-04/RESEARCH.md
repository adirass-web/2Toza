# Public research selected for Pricing-04

14 October 2026, Asia/Jerusalem. These sources were searched and read for this pass. This is a research-based proposal, not evidence of improved Toza sales or customer comprehension. Public guidance remains subordinate to owner instructions and approved service facts.

## Choices made for this service

The four-cell flow explains sequence but hardly shows what the client buys. Replace it with a small number of substantial scenes: contact; a model of relevant digital access and an ordered risk summary; then agreed implementation, compatible keys, testing and OpSec practice. Keep the separate decision after Assessment clear without giving it the same visual weight as an entire paid service.

Use counted/grouped people and devices, model-neutral hardware-key drawings, explicit hours/days, a simplified threat-model illustration and examples of practical habits. Short labels carry meaning. The illustration must demonstrate the method, not present fictional findings as a real case or promise a new formal report.

The owner named “yubiko keys”; the recognizable spelling is Yubico/YubiKey. [Yubico's product family and example product](https://www.yubico.com/product/yubikey-5-series/yubikey-5c-nfc/) distinguish form factors and protocols. Public vendor information does not establish which SKU Toza supplies. Use a model-neutral proposed brand label, compatible-key counts and a representative silhouette; do not promise USB/NFC/FIPS features or universal account compatibility.

The CRE planner is selected over a generic diagram of identical boxes because it asks what benefit makes each next action worthwhile. Its newsletter/forum examples do not fit Toza's private service. StoryBrand was considered in search, but not selected as the main visual method: a simple three-step plan does not by itself solve the current lack of tangible service value. No complete StoryBrand book was read.

Generic recommended-tier badges, fabricated social proof, numerical conversion claims, guarantees, scarcity, lead magnets, automated checkout and unnecessary data capture do not follow from these sources. The present action remains the existing neutral WhatsApp/Signal private conversation. Paid work still follows written offer, acceptance and payment; full hardening requires separate acceptance.

## Evaluation

Review whether a prospect can identify, without decoding a paragraph: first action, first fee and deliverable; main hardening fee; conditional remaining balance; one/four people; device/key counts; personal work hours versus Assessment time; included network difference; threat model and OpSec teaching; and the separate decision after Assessment. Then track qualified enquiries, paid Assessments and subsequent full engagements against a recorded baseline. Visual polish and expert review alone establish neither comprehension nor sales uplift.

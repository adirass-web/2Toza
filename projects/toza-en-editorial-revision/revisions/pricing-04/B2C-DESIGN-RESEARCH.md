## The common pattern worth using

All three text structures separate a short choice from a fuller specification. They lead with an audience, quantity or recognizable included bundle, associate a price with its real charging terms and give the same understandable next action at meaningful points. None of that requires enlarging every feature into a decorative illustration.

For Toza, the difference is coverage: one person versus four, with the same individual standard and the specified network extension. The buyer still contacts Toza before a written offer. A paid Assessment establishes the risks; separately accepted hardening implements the agreed changes and teaches their use. Conditional credit is a purchase rule, not promotional pricing or a weaker protection tier.

## Specific proposal for compressing the current Pricing-04 rendering

The first tangible prototype is more informative visually, but initial browser measurements of complete page height (`document.documentElement.scrollHeight`, including header/footer) were 5,922px desktop and 10,614px at 320px. Pricing-03 was materially shorter at the same metric: 3,963px desktop, 6,473px at 390px and 7,440px at 320px. Larger method/practice scenes plus complete visible narrative duplicate much of the information; the owner has already objected to a long Pricing page. The exact current output measurements are in `preview/checks.json`; these initial values are not a frozen final comparison after later refinements.

1. **Keep one compact core decision surface.** Retain the exact seven core rows and full prices. Reduce repeated decorative objects and vertical padding; keep one/four people, four pairs of devices and four pairs of keys recognizable. Devices and supplied keys remain separate. Each row keeps short authoritative text. Preserve mobile SBS and 16px cells; shrink spacing and object footprint, not words or type legibility. A second set of tall plan cards is unnecessary unless it replaces the comparison; adding it would increase repetition.
2. **Common facts once, precise full specifications second.** Keep shared hardening, contingent migration, OpSec and verification together in a compact common section. Do not repeat that entire list inside the later implementation illustration. The full specification can remain a native disclosure, with meaningful direct links and unchanged complete text.
3. **One compact method illustration with a summary attached.** The Assessment should show circumstances/information → relevant access → risks/priorities, and a practical summary. Recompose it into a smaller integrated scene rather than a large map followed by a second large sheet on phones. Keep the method-example caption, ₪3,600/typically-four-hours label, practical summary whether or not continuing, safe-first-aid/completion and initial scope visible. Detailed narrative that repeats the graphic can become clearly labelled secondary content only if its exact label is approved and source order/fidelity remain verifiable.
4. **Implementation plus practice, not a second feature catalogue.** The later scene can be a concise per-person device/key strip paired with four small, labelled practice situations. Exact shared teaching topics and verification/record labels remain visible, but do not need large boxes and a repeated six-item list when the shared inclusion section already states the same work. Keep the separate owner decision and aftercare association unmistakable. No arrow crosses the decision as an automatic next stage.
5. **Keep amounts and eligibility locally together.** The exact unchanged credit-condition paragraph is now repeated immediately after the core matrix as authorized by the root, so the conditional remaining amounts have local context as well as the direct-entry rule above. That added wording serves a material purchase condition. A second full ledger graphic would duplicate the same price calculation; avoid it unless it demonstrably resolves comprehension better than the existing row.
6. **Use one action consistently.** Contact through the existing neutral WhatsApp/Signal destinations. Keep an immediate action and repeat it after the meaningful Assessment/protection explanation, rather than turning each visual object into a button. No booking/payment UI is added before written offer and acceptance.

These are implementation recommendations, not new public copy or service facts. The frozen Pricing and visual inputs remain unchanged. The exact credit condition was repeated after the matrix; the root then authorized the compact implementation. Final measured reductions and limits are recorded in `DESIGN.md`. Any compression must still preserve all source text, public anchors, full specifications, client control, separate paid-stage acceptance and necessary price conditions.

## How to judge the adaptation

Use screenshots for geometry, not as proof of commercial value. Aim for a substantially shorter main decision path than the initial expanded Pricing-04 render while preserving 320px readability; report measured final heights and visible words rather than promising a particular percentage. Ask a reader to locate full price, first payment, eligible remainder and conditions; distinguish worked-on devices from supplied keys; separate Assessment time from hardening time; identify what the threat model produces and what OpSec practice teaches; explain the optional second purchase. Track qualified conversations and paid stages after a separately approved production release.

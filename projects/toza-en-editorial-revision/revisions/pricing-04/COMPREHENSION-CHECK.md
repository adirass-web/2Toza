# Pricing-04 comprehension and commercial evaluation

This is a proposed evaluation guide, not completed customer testing. No interviews, outreach, contact-message tests or new tracking have been performed.

Show the prototype to a suitable prospective client at their normal device size. Ask them to explain the offer before explaining it yourself. Record their own answers and uncertainty. Separate errors from preferences.

| Question | Accurate understanding to check |
| --- | --- |
| What is your next step, and what happens before any technical work? | Neutral private WhatsApp/Signal conversation checks fit and urgency without a purchase commitment; accepted written offer and payment precede Assessment scheduling. |
| What does the first ₪3,600 purchase give you? | Initial client's specified two-device review, personal threat model, specific risk assessment and ordered next steps in the existing practical summary; summary is retained without continuation. Safely possible approved urgent first aid and agreed outcome completion are included. |
| What are the two full-work choices and their prices? | Personal Shield: one person, fixed ₪14,000; Inner Circle: four people, from ₪42,000. Same individual standard; VAT included; agreed additions separate. |
| Which objects are worked on, and which are supplied? | Each person's main phone plus PC/iPad/second phone is worked on. Two compatible physical keys per person are supplied/configured/tested. The proposal names Yubico without specifying a model. Inner Circle also includes one shared network/router beyond eight personal devices. |
| How do time and remaining payments work? | Assessment typically four hours; Personal work typically 7–14 hours excluding Assessment; Inner typically 2–4 working days. Eligible remaining balances ₪10,400/from ₪38,400 require full work to begin within 14 days of initial-service completion. Booking alone is insufficient; credit once per group; additions separate. |
| What makes full work different from Assessment, and is it automatic? | Assessment establishes the relevant risks/priorities. Full work separately implements agreed hardening/essential migration where needed, tests material changes, supplies/configures keys, teaches changed setup and practical OpSec, and provides a private dated record and limited adjustment support. Separate acceptance is required; continuation is voluntary. |

Also ask: “What would you still need to know before contacting Toza or accepting the Assessment?” This can reveal an objection or missing practical detail without leading the reader to praise the design.

Record whether the illustrative method was mistaken for a real finding or promised new formal report; whether the four-person scene was mistaken for four Assessments within the initial fee; and whether a time figure looked like a guarantee or automatic cutoff.

## Commercial measurement after an approved release

Record a comparable baseline of qualified enquiries, accepted paid Assessments, separately accepted full engagements and revenue. Preserve source mix and observation periods in the comparison. With low traffic, report counts and recurring objections rather than unstable percentage improvements. An increase in clicks alone is not success.

No comprehension, conversion or sales improvement has been established by expert review, shorter page height or browser checks. A favourable result must also preserve accurate understanding of scope, payment and consent.

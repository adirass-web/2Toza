## Conversion plan: make each next action useful

This is an internal planning table, not a recommendation to add more customer-facing boxes. Prospect needs below are professional hypotheses to test, not interview findings.

| Decision | What the prospect wants | Business goal | Why the next action is worthwhile |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Request a private conversation | Determine fit, practical arrangements and urgency without sending sensitive evidence or committing to buy | Qualified enquiries with correct price/scope expectations | Personal fit/urgency clarification and an intelligible written-offer path. No free technical diagnosis or invented free risk report. |
| Accept the paid Assessment | Know relevant exposure and priorities before committing to broader work; understand time/devices and the first fee | Suitable, paid and completed Assessments | A personal threat model, specific risk assessment and ordered next steps in the existing practical summary; approved first aid where safely possible. The main page can show three substantial service scenes with an evident separate decision, rather than four equal labelled cells. The Assessment scene should show how context/access become priorities; the hardening scene should show implemented settings, supplied keys and practice. A concise primary contact action can recur at a useful decision point, while remaining the same neutral WhatsApp/Signal qualification route. Do not turn stage illustrations into paid-checkout actions.

## Visual fact and authority constraints sent to the copy expert

- **Devices versus equipment:** show devices worked on, not supplied phones/computers. One person has one main phone plus one PC, iPad or second phone; four people have that same allowance each, eight personal devices. The group network/router is additional.
- **Keys:** two compatible keys supplied/configured/tested for Personal; two per person, eight for Inner. The owner requested Yubico/YubiKey specificity as proposal direction. A model-neutral vendor label does not authorize a SKU, connector, NFC/FIPS/protocol feature or universal compatibility promise. A specific product image can imply those features even if its caption is vague.
- **Assessment scope:** the initial paid visit remains the initial client/two-device Assessment. Remaining-group review/mapping belongs to the separately bought group work. A group illustration must not imply the first ₪3,600 inspects all four people/eight devices.
- **Threat model:** illustrate method/categories/relationships, not fake client findings, a numeric risk score, confirmed intrusion or a new formal report. “Illustration of the method” is sufficient; no repetitive alarmist disclaimers are needed.
- **OpSec:** make already authorized practical teaching tangible through the changed setup, signing in, recovery, sharing and permissions. No covert-surveillance training, attack exercise, new course/certification or unlimited follow-up is implied.
- **Time:** Personal's typical 7–14 work hours exclude Assessment, with the second working day included if needed. Assessment typically four hours remains outcome-based; Inner typically 2–4 working days is not 7–14 hours for the whole group or a consecutive-day guarantee.
- **Money:** keep VAT, full prices, initial fee, conditional balances and the 14-day commencement/not-booking condition next to the relevant amount and at direct-entry comparison views. Personal base price is fixed; Inner is a starting price; credit once per group.
- **Common inclusions:** sharing one treatment for hardening, contingent essential migration, teaching, verification and records is appropriate. Preserve group-specific records/privacy, included-network support and group-completion support clock in accessible complete terms; common badges must not contradict those distinctions.

## Evaluation

A reader should be able to identify the first action and paid benefit, the full price and conditional balance, person/device/key quantities, time units, network difference, threat-model purpose, practical OpSec benefit and voluntary second purchase. Record wrong answers and uncertainties; pleasing graphics and fewer paragraphs are insufficient evidence.

Business measurement remains qualified enquiries, suitable paid Assessments and separately accepted full services, using explicit windows/cohorts and the approved baseline plan. Do not count every contact click as an enquiry or every Assessment as a likely full-service sale. No new mandatory customer-study gate, external outreach or analytics implementation follows from this research review.

**Disposition:** proceed to exact-source and design review under the Toza workflow. The selection is proportionate, its limitations are correctly declared, and its main opportunity is showing concrete purchased value without reducing material scope or creating new promises.

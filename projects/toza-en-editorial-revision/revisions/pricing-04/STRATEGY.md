## Architecture and reading priority

1. **Visible main offer:** Personal Shield, ₪14,000, one person/two devices, senior in-person delivery and private-conversation CTA.
2. **Visible choice:** seven meaningful comparison rows: price, people, devices, supplied keys, shared network, work time, conditional remaining payment. Keep the separate ₪3,600 Assessment and full start-within-14-days/not-booking/once-group/additions qualification before the table, including for direct #full-services entry.
3. **Visible common value:** agreed hardening/essential migration, OpSec teaching, testing/record. Shared inclusions once, rather than in two service columns.
4. **Visible prospect journey:** substantial scenes for contact action and fit/urgency; paid Assessment with illustrated inputs → personal threat model → practical risk/priorities summary; explicit decision; agreed implementation with supplied hardware, tested changes and practical habits. Safe urgent first aid, outcome completion and summary without continuation stay visible. Decision is an annotation between paid stages, not a fourth equally weighted service box.
5. **Accessible full reference:** complete scope, work-time/day inclusions, training/testing, mapping, records, consent, support, network/migration limits, credit detail, bespoke/extras, urgent terms, follow-up and boundaries. Every previous comparison fact remains available. Never hide a material qualifier away from the amount it qualifies.
6. **Contact:** existing neutral WhatsApp/Signal action and FAQ privacy guidance; no new intake form, checkout or booking system.

**Density cuts:** eliminate repeated stage labels, duplicate method matrices and long introductory descriptions; show counts and object labels rather than narrating them. Full reference is intentionally retained for informed choice. The full reference repeats essential comparison facts to remain comprehensible when opened independently, without becoming the default reading path.

**CTA logic:** private conversation → accepted written Assessment offer/payment → risk summary → voluntary separate full-service acceptance. Contact performs qualification, not inspection or diagnosis. Show the benefit at each stage. No purchase obligation or automatic progression.

**Required boundaries:** all prices/VAT; fixed versus from; allowed device types; initial client versus group mapping; compatible supplied key counts; typical time versus cap; first payment versus eligible balance; support limits; personal consent; authorized work; honest completion; urgency trigger and replacement surcharge.

**Do not say:** guaranteed privacy, every risk found, formal threat-model certification/report, stronger Inner security tier, eight arbitrary devices, hardware supplied with client devices, agency endorsement, automatic credit, contact-triggered urgent arrival, technical work in first conversation.

**Open fact item:** proposed Yubico supply label is attributable to the latest owner request, not to accepted marketing-02 or vendor research. No model-specific claim is proposed.

Ten sweeps performed before independent review: clarity, voice, relevance, proof, specificity, emotional effect, friction, prestige, compression and human credibility. Principal controlled tradeoff: the public page retains substantial detailed reference while making the default reading path shorter and more tangible. Actual comprehension and qualified-client conversion must be measured; expert review alone cannot prove uplift.

# Pricing-04 conversion plan

The customer-facing journey should make each paid step worth understanding. This worksheet adapts the three questions in the [CRE Funnel Planner](https://conversion-rate-experts.com/conversion-funnel/). It is a design hypothesis based on current Toza facts and owner direction, not observed customer research.

| Stage | What the prospect needs | Toza's goal | What to show | Actual next action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Arrive and understand the offer | Know whether this is personal protection and what it costs | A qualified visitor recognizes Personal Shield or the four-person scope | Full price, counted people/devices/keys, practical outcomes and initial Assessment fee | Request a private conversation |
| Private conversation | Check fit, urgency and practical arrangements without committing | Suitable enquiry that can receive a written Assessment offer | Familiar contact-channel controls, short neutral-message guidance, clearly nontechnical qualification | Send the existing neutral WhatsApp or Signal message; Toza replies personally |
| Paid Assessment | Understand relevant risks and what should change | Accepted paid Assessment, completed under agreed scope | Client devices/account connections becoming a personal threat model, specific risk assessment and ordered priorities in the practical summary; typically four hours, ₪3,600 | Written offer, explicit acceptance and receipt of payment before scheduling |
| Separate decision | Know what full work would do and how the fee/credit works | Suitable client elects and accepts full hardening | The risk priorities lead to agreed changes; full prices and the conditional remaining balances remain together | Accept the separately defined full engagement if chosen; no automatic continuation |
| Hardening and use | Obtain the tailored setup and learn to use it | Deliver the agreed paid full engagement | Configured keys, relevant access changes, essential migration where needed, tests and OpSec practice; hours/days and included support | In-person implementation with the client present and approving material changes |

The graphic can use three substantial scenes (contact, Assessment, full implementation) with a clear decision/acceptance connector. It need not display five identical cells simply because this internal worksheet has five rows. Show objects, relationships and received value. Put the first real contact action in or beside the journey, so the diagram helps someone act.

The first paid Assessment covers the initial client's specified two devices. Group review and mapping for other participants belong to the separately purchased Inner Circle engagement. A visual of four people must not suggest that the initial ₪3,600 covers four Assessments. The threat-model illustration demonstrates a method; the approved practical summary remains the deliverable.

OpSec teaching belongs to full work and concerns using the changed setup, signing in, recovering access, sharing information and granting permissions. It is not a new stand-alone course. Device figures show coverage; the security keys are supplied hardware.

## Measurements

Use the reviewed Pricing-03 prototype and the current published page as recorded design baselines. Before release, test whether a representative reader can identify the next action, the two paid stages, the Assessment outcome, the full versus conditional remaining prices, counts and time units from the graphic and short labels. Ask them to explain the credit condition and whether full work is automatic. Record mistakes and uncertainties, not an invented uplift percentage.

After an approved release, use existing aggregate information and ordinary qualification records to compare suitable enquiries, accepted paid Assessments, subsequent paid hardening and revenue with a comparable baseline/source mix. For limited traffic, report counts and recurring objections. This prototype adds no tracking or outreach and does not test-send contact messages.

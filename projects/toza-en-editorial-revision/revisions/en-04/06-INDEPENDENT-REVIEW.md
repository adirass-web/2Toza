**Correction verified:** The final sentence uses **“the original concern.”** Useful current-access work remains the point; no additional warning paragraph was needed.

**Dimensions:** credibility, emotional precision, authority. **Owner factual decision:** none. No attribution or proven-compromise implication remains in this passage.

**Correction verified:** The initial-visit link and unchanged urgent-availability sentence now follow the Assessment summary, before the full-hardening H2. The full-services/prices/credit link follows the Personal Shield paragraph.

**Dimensions:** clarity, conversion friction, service consistency. **Owner factual decision:** none. No fee, urgency condition or service inclusion changed.

# Make your digital life more private, with work tailored to you.

Private information can become leverage over your relationships, money or reputation. You do not need to know which setting or account deserves attention before asking for help.

Dr. Lior Tabansky works with you, in person. The paid Assessment examines relevant access and addresses approved urgent problems within scope. A separately agreed full engagement carries out broader hardening and teaching, tailored to your circumstances.

## Before you book

Start with a private conversation about your situation. We establish whether Toza fits and discuss urgency. This conversation includes no device inspection or technical work and does not commit you to purchase.

You then receive a written scope and price. After your acceptance and receipt of payment, we schedule the Assessment.

## Your first paid visit: inspection, diagnosis and urgent first aid

**Private Exposure Assessment · Typically four hours**

**₪3,600 including VAT**

This is hands-on work with you, on one main phone and one other device: a PC, iPad or second phone. Dr. Tabansky inspects relevant identities, accounts, apps, permissions and recovery routes, including access to messages, photos, documents and location information.

- **Understand what the access could mean for you.** Dr. Tabansky examines how the relevant devices, identities and services connect, what they can reveal or allow in your circumstances, and which changes should come first. You receive an interpretation of your own setup, including connections you may not have known to ask about.
- **Have urgent problems addressed during the visit.** Where urgent exposure can safely be fixed within the agreed scope, Dr. Tabansky explains the change and makes it with your approval.
- **Receive a practical summary.** See what was reviewed, what was found, what changed, what remains and the recommended next steps. You receive this even if you buy nothing further.

The visit completes the agreed intake, access mapping, diagnosis and approved urgent first aid.

**[See the initial visit in detail](/en/private-exposure-assessment.html)**

## Hardening and practical security habits, fitted to your life

Personal Shield and Inner Circle Shield put the agreed hardening into practice across your devices, digital identities, accounts, apps and data access. This can include closing unwanted access, strengthening sign-in and recovery, and changing how information is shared. Dr. Tabansky teaches you how to use the resulting setup and the practical security habits suited to your circumstances.

Both services include essential migration where needed, physical security-key setup and verification. The full engagement gives deliberate attention to the agreed setup, beyond the Assessment's urgent first aid. Its value is not limited to correcting a known incident. We set out the proposed work, scope and price; you decide whether to continue. There is no automatic continuation.

Toza does not monitor your devices or install security software on them.

### Personal Shield: hardening fitted to your circumstances

The Assessment findings guide the work on your agreed setup. Dr. Tabansky tests material changes and shows you how to use them, including how to recover access. You work through the relevant security practices with him, so you can make informed choices when sharing information or granting access in daily life. Your private dated record gives you a reference for the work performed.

The included adjustment support covers issues directly related to our changes through WhatsApp, Signal and scheduled calls. It does not involve remote control of your devices.

### Inner Circle Shield: care for four people, with individual privacy

Four people receive coordinated hardening under one agreed engagement, with the same standard of device and account hardening, essential migration, training and verification as Personal Shield for each person. The initial ₪3,600 visit covers the initial client's two devices and relevant accounts. Review and mapping for the remaining group are included in the separately purchased Inner Circle engagement.

The included shared network and router receive review and hardening of accessible settings and authorized administration. Replacement hardware, work on additional connected devices and additional locations are quoted separately. We agree the scope, schedule and total price in writing before you accept.

Each participant receives a private dated record. The shared-network work is documented separately. Personal details are shared only with that person's consent; the group engagement does not give participants access to one another's information.

## What the service covers

Toza helps you strengthen the security and privacy of your personal digital life. You can come with a concern, a change in circumstances, or a wish to move beyond the defaults. Separation, business and inheritance disputes are among the situations where access to private information can have serious consequences. We work within your authorized digital environment, on relevant access identified together. This is not a promise to discover every account or remove every possible exposure.

Essential migration covers identity, recovery and critical-service access. Historical archives and bulk data migration require a separately agreed scope. The service does not include investigation of another person, forensic reconstruction, legal advice or routine IT support. The dated record documents the work; it is not a forensic report, evidence document or security guarantee.

You are present and control your credentials. Toza does not keep them for ongoing use or remotely control your devices. Material changes require your approval. [Read how your information is handled](/en/faq.html#privacy).

## Recommendation

Use the direct Home opening and the more personal Pricing opening. Home needs to make an unfamiliar risk understandable; Pricing needs to make expert work and its benefit worth considering. Quiet premium character comes from precise language, individual judgment and direct personal delivery. It does not require hiding the consequence.

The public copy does not retell the owner's sensitive cases. It uses the supported distinctions behind them: access through ordinary arrangements, personal knowledge and trust, connections that persist, and copies outside current accounts. This creates recognition without an attack recipe, accusation or invented case study.

## Home: recommended opening

**Personal digital security and privacy**

**Private information can be used against you.**

A private conversation, financial information or plans you have not shared publicly can be used to pressure you, cost you money or damage your reputation. The access may come from ordinary arrangements you have never had reason to question.

Dr. Lior Tabansky works with you, in person, to assess relevant exposure and carry out agreed technical changes. You can choose a more secure, more private digital life before you notice a problem. You do not have to work out where to look first.

**Request a private conversation**

**H-01: quieter alternative headline, same body**

> Your private information can become someone else's leverage.

The alternative makes the loss of power explicit and sounds more reserved. The recommended line is easier to grasp immediately. “Leverage” may require interpretation. Neither has been tested with prospective clients.

## Home: the substantive changes

| Accepted wording or treatment | Proposed change | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| “Your private life deserves more care than a default setting.” | “Private information can be used against you.” | Moves from deserving care to understanding the possible consequence. |
| Ordinary features and connected devices, described generally | Personal knowledge, shared permissions, information leading to further access, and copies stored elsewhere | Explains why the reader may have no visible warning and why a simple account change is not the whole issue. |
| “They may not need to hack you.” | “Everyday connections can reveal more than you expect.” | Keeps the awareness-building point without relying on an ambiguous definition of hacking. |
| “Stronger security, with help using it” | “Close unwanted access. Build stronger security habits.” | Names intervention and a practical benefit. The scoped body explains what work can include. |
| Relevant practices and using changes | Responding to approaches, sharing information, granting access, sign-in and recovery | Makes teaching part of security, beyond learning a new setup. |
| The same person learns the situation and does the work | Explains how private priorities, relationships and dependencies guide personal threat modelling, then connects that judgment to the person making the changes | Shows the bespoke character through the work itself. No formal new report is promised. |
| Two Assessment explanations in separate sections | One complete section with fee, duration, scope, inspection, approved urgent first aid and practical summary | Removes duplication while preserving the independent value of the first paid service. |
| Separation as a wish for greater privacy | Private messages and plans as a possible source of pressure | Gives that situation a concrete human stake without asserting misconduct. |

## Pricing: recommended opening

**Make your digital life more private, with work tailored to you.**

Private information can become leverage over your relationships, money or reputation. You do not need to know which setting or account deserves attention before asking for help.

Dr. Lior Tabansky works with you, in person. The paid Assessment examines relevant access and addresses approved urgent problems within scope. A separately agreed full engagement carries out broader hardening and teaching, tailored to your circumstances.

**P-H1-B: more action-led alternative headline, same body**

> Understand the risks. Make the changes with Dr. Tabansky.

This foregrounds action and direct delivery. The recommended version gives the client benefit and personal fit more prominence. Both require the stage distinction immediately below them.

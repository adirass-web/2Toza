## Ten-sweep disposition

1. Clarity: explain older copies; distinguish digital identity and the separate service stages.
2. Voice: calm declarative statements; warmth through personal attention and an easy first contact. No luxury adjectives, craft metaphors or alarmist escalation.
3. Relevance: Home earns awareness and contact; Pricing explains paid work and benefit. Avoid another risk inventory on Pricing.
4. Proof: use owner-reported mechanism categories and current authorized service actions. No public case study, prevalence claim, exclusive capability or invented success.
5. Specificity: recovery, sharing, access, teaching and testing show the work. Consequences include pressure, money and reputation without attack instructions.
6. Emotional effect: connect risk to available action. Do not tell the reader that someone is spying or that a relationship is adversarial.
7. Friction: retain neutral first message, private conversation, written terms, acceptance/payment, Assessment value and voluntary continuation.
8. Prestige: founder delivery, individual judgment and doing the work with the client establish the character of the practice.
9. Compression: replace weak passages; consolidate the Home Assessment explanation. Measure net length instead of claiming these are shorter pages.
10. Human credibility: remove awkward abstractions; retain direct ordinary language. Keep variants as replacement units, not additional copy.

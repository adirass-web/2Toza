## The Assessment establishes your risks and the work {#assessment}

**Private Exposure Assessment · ₪3,600 including VAT · Typically four hours**

Toza begins with your circumstances and the information you need to protect. We develop your personal threat model by examining relevant apps, devices, accounts, access arrangements and digital traces. The result is a specific risk assessment and priorities for hardening, identity/recovery changes and operational security practices.

The visit covers one main phone and one PC, iPad or second phone, with relevant identities, accounts, apps, permissions and recovery routes, including access to messages, photos, documents and location information. Review stays within what is disclosed, observable or otherwise lawfully accessible in your authorized environment and agreed scope.

You receive the risk assessment, priorities and ordered next steps in a practical summary of what was reviewed, found and changed and what remains. This establishes the plan for the full protection work. You receive the summary whether or not you continue.

If urgent exposure is identified, Toza will make the approved first-aid changes that can safely be completed within the visit's agreed scope. The Assessment completes the agreed intake, mapping, diagnosis and approved urgent first aid. Typically four hours is not an automatic cutoff or a trigger for another purchase.

## From the risk assessment to the hardened setup

### Personal Shield

Toza will strengthen the agreed settings and access arrangements around your threat model. Where needed, we establish new core accounts and migrate essential identity, recovery and critical-service access. We supply, configure and test two compatible physical security keys, test material changes and verify the applicable work against the checklist.

Personal teaching covers the changed setup and practical security habits: signing in, recovering access, sharing information and granting permissions. The private dated record gives you a reference for the work. A technically blocked or declined action is not presented as completed.

A typical Personal Shield engagement requires 7–14 hours of in-person work, excluding the initial Assessment. The price includes one primary working day and a second if needed. Fourteen days of limited adjustment support cover issues directly related to Toza's changes through WhatsApp, Signal and scheduled calls; this does not involve remote control of your devices.

### Inner Circle Shield

The initial ₪3,600 Assessment covers the initial client's two devices and relevant accounts. Review and mapping for the remaining group are included in the separately purchased Inner Circle engagement. Each person receives the same standard of hardening, essential migration, teaching and verification as Personal Shield, with two compatible security keys each, eight in total.

One shared home or home-office network and router are included in addition to the eight personal devices. We review and harden accessible settings and authorized administration. Replacement hardware, additional connected devices and additional locations are quoted separately. Scope, schedule and the final group price are confirmed in writing before acceptance.

## Service boundaries {#service-details}

Toza works within your authorized digital environment and agreed scope, on relevant access identified together. The service does not promise to discover every account or find and remove every exposure. A concern or current access path does not establish wrongdoing or past use; changing access cannot recall an independent copy already held elsewhere.

Toza does not investigate another person, reconstruct events forensically, collect legal evidence, give legal advice or provide routine IT support. The dated record documents work; it is not a forensic report, evidence document, certification or security guarantee.

You remain present, control your credentials and approve material changes. Toza does not keep your credentials for ongoing use, remotely control or monitor your devices, or install security software on them. Your situation is handled confidentially.

# Check the Pricing decision, not just its appearance

Use representative prospective clients. Show either the current published Pricing or the new proposal, with source/order recorded. Avoid teaching a person the answers on the first version and then treating their second attempt as an independent improvement.

Ask the person to use the page to explain:

1. What would Toza do for one person, and what is the Personal Shield full-engagement price?
2. What would you buy and pay for first? What do you receive from that visit?
3. When is the ₪10,400 remaining payment applicable? Does booking within fourteen days qualify?
4. What changes for four people? How many personal devices and physical keys are included, and what network is included?
5. What decision follows the Assessment? What happens if you do not buy the full engagement?

Record their answer, whether they found it without help, any confusion, and the section they used. Separately ask which scope fits their situation and whether the next step is clear. Do not ask them to disclose sensitive case content or contact Toza as part of the test.

## Answer key

- Personal: tailored agreed hardening, essential migration where needed, two compatible supplied/configured/tested keys, teaching, verification and private dated record; one person and the stated two-device allowance; ₪14,000 including VAT. Additions are separate.
- First paid service: separate ₪3,600 VAT-inclusive Private Exposure Assessment, typically four hours with outcome-based completion; personal threat model, relevant device/app/account/access review, specific risk assessment and ordered next steps in a practical summary, plus approved safe urgent first aid where needed/in scope.
- Credit: full engagement begins within fourteen days of completion of the initial service; booking alone is insufficient. Personal remaining balance is ₪10,400 only when eligible; the full-service fee remains ₪14,000. Group credit applies once, with ₪38,400 remaining at its starting scope.
- Inner Circle: four people; two specified personal devices each, eight total; two compatible keys each, eight total; one shared home/home-office network and router beyond those devices. The same individual protection standard, from ₪42,000 VAT-inclusive; final scope, schedule and price confirmed in writing.
- Continuation: a separate client decision and acceptance of full work. The client receives the Assessment summary whether or not they continue; the visit is not an automatic second purchase.

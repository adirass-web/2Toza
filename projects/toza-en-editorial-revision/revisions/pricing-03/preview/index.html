Toza develops your personal threat model by examining relevant apps, devices, accounts, access arrangements and digital traces. What the protection work includes

Toza hardens agreed device, identity, account, app and data-access settings around your threat model. Where needed, we establish new core accounts and migrate essential identity, recovery and critical-service access. Historical archives and bulk data migration require a separately agreed scope.

We supply, configure and test the included compatible physical security keys. Personal teaching covers the changed setup, signing in, recovering access, sharing information and granting permissions. We test material changes and verify applicable work against the checklist. A technically blocked or declined action is not presented as completed.

For Inner Circle, network work covers accessible settings and authorized administration. Each person's record stays private; personal details are shared only with that person's consent. The group engagement does not give participants access to one another's information.

Limited adjustment support covers issues directly related to Toza's changes through WhatsApp, Signal and scheduled calls, without remote control of your devices. The included periods are shown in the comparison above.

Different scope and additional work

Bespoke Private Protection — individually agreed scope and price. For severe, unusual or highly complex situations requiring different work, we set out the scope and price after the initial visit for your decision.

Extra people or devices, network work beyond the included scope, replacement hardware, additional connected devices or locations, and vehicle access reviews are quoted individually. An expansion needs your approval before work begins.

Service boundaries

Toza works within your authorized digital environment and agreed scope, using what is disclosed, observable or otherwise lawfully accessible. The service does not promise to discover every account or find and remove every exposure. An access path does not establish wrongdoing or past use; changing access cannot recall independent copies already held elsewhere.

Toza does not investigate another person, reconstruct events forensically, collect legal evidence, give legal advice or provide routine IT support. The dated record documents work; it is not a forensic report, evidence document, certification or security guarantee.

You remain present, control your credentials and approve material changes. Toza does not keep credentials for ongoing use, remotely control or monitor devices, or install security software. Your situation is handled confidentially. How your information is handled · Detailed service boundaries

## From conversation to protection

### 1. Check fit privately

The first conversation checks your situation, urgency and practical arrangements. It includes no device inspection, mapping, diagnosis or technical work and does not commit you to purchase. You receive a written offer; after your explicit acceptance and receipt of payment, we schedule the Assessment.

### 2. Establish the risks {#assessment}

**Private Exposure Assessment · ₪3,600 including VAT · Typically four hours**

Toza develops your personal threat model by examining relevant apps, devices, accounts, access arrangements and digital traces. The visit covers one main phone and one PC, iPad or second phone, including relevant identities, permissions, recovery routes and access to messages, photos, documents and location information.

You receive a specific risk assessment, priorities and ordered next steps in a practical summary: what was reviewed, found and changed, and what remains. It sets the priorities for hardening, identity/recovery changes and operational security practices. You receive it whether or not you continue.

If urgent exposure is identified, Toza will make the approved first-aid changes that can safely be completed within the agreed scope. Completion covers the agreed intake, mapping, diagnosis and approved urgent first aid. Four hours is not an automatic cutoff or a trigger for another purchase.

You decide whether to proceed with Personal Shield, Inner Circle Shield or a different agreed scope. Broader protection requires its own acceptance; there is no automatic continuation.

## What the protection work includes

Toza hardens agreed device, identity, account, app and data-access settings around your threat model. Where needed, we establish new core accounts and migrate essential identity, recovery and critical-service access. Historical archives and bulk data migration require a separately agreed scope.

We supply, configure and test the included compatible physical security keys. Personal teaching covers the changed setup, signing in, recovering access, sharing information and granting permissions. We test material changes and verify applicable work against the checklist. A technically blocked or declined action is not presented as completed.

For Inner Circle, network work covers accessible settings and authorized administration. Each person's record stays private; personal details are shared only with that person's consent. The group engagement does not give participants access to one another's information.

Limited adjustment support covers issues directly related to Toza's changes through WhatsApp, Signal and scheduled calls, without remote control of your devices. The included periods are shown in the comparison above.

## Service boundaries {#service-details}

Toza works within your authorized digital environment and agreed scope, using what is disclosed, observable or otherwise lawfully accessible. The service does not promise to discover every account or find and remove every exposure. An access path does not establish wrongdoing or past use; changing access cannot recall independent copies already held elsewhere.

Toza does not investigate another person, reconstruct events forensically, collect legal evidence, give legal advice or provide routine IT support. The dated record documents work; it is not a forensic report, evidence document, certification or security guarantee.

You remain present, control your credentials and approve material changes. Toza does not keep credentials for ongoing use, remotely control or monitor devices, or install security software. Your situation is handled confidentially. [How your information is handled](/en/faq.html#privacy) · [Detailed service boundaries](/en/faq.html#boundaries)

# Your private life deserves more care than a default setting.

You carry private conversations and years of personal history in your pocket. Who can still reach them may depend on arrangements you have barely thought about since they were set up.

Perhaps something feels wrong. Or you simply want your privacy to get the same care you give to other important parts of your life. You do not have to figure it out alone.

Dr. Lior Tabansky works with you, in person, to understand what matters and carry out the agreed technical work. You can start without knowing which account, setting or past decision needs attention.

**[Request a private conversation](#contact)**

In person · With you present · One senior expert, start to finish

## They may not need to hack you.

Private information can be reached through many kinds of access, including ordinary features that connect devices and accounts. A service you once shared or an old device can remain part of a wider set of connections. The presence of that access does not mean someone has misused it.

Dr. Tabansky looks at how your relevant devices, digital identities, accounts, apps and data connect. He explains what those arrangements allow in your circumstances and what a change would affect. You get help with the questions you may not have known to ask.

## Have someone work through the details with you

The paid Private Exposure Assessment includes hands-on inspection of relevant devices and access, diagnosis, and approved urgent first-aid fixes where safely possible within the agreed scope. Dr. Tabansky explains what he finds and what he proposes to change. You receive a practical summary and next steps, even if you buy nothing further.

## Stronger security, with help using it

Personal Shield and Inner Circle Shield harden the agreed settings and access arrangements around your life. You learn practical security habits and how to use the changes, with personal teaching and testing of material changes. You can give your security and privacy this attention without waiting for a known incident.

Each full engagement has a separately agreed scope, price and purchase. Toza does not monitor your devices or install security software on them.

The person who learns your situation does the technical work with you. [Why I created Toza](/en/why-us.html) · [How Toza helps](/en/what-happens-during-the-visit.html)

## Your confidence matters. Your approval directs the work.

Your situation and the information you share are handled confidentially. You stay present, approve material changes and control your credentials. Toza does not keep them for ongoing use or remotely control your devices.

Work stays within your authorized environment and agreed scope. We do not promise to find or remove every exposure. We do not investigate another person, reconstruct events forensically, collect legal evidence or give legal advice.

[Privacy and service boundaries](/en/faq.html) · [How Toza differs from other help](/en/faq.html#how-toza-differs)

## You can ask for help before you have answers. {#contact}

You do not need to explain everything in the first message. Send a short, neutral WhatsApp or Signal message. Dr. Tabansky replies personally as soon as possible. This conversation checks fit and urgency; it includes no device inspection, mapping, diagnosis or technical work. Contacting Toza does not commit you to purchase.

If the service fits, you receive a written scope and price. The paid visit is scheduled after your explicit acceptance and receipt of payment.

Suggested message: **“I'd like a private conversation. Please let me know the next step.”**

Do not send passwords, recovery codes, screenshots, legal documents, intimate material or details, or detailed allegations in the first message.

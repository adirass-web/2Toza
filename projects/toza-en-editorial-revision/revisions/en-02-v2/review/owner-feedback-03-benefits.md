## Strategy

Common reader arrival: an intelligent private client may have only a hunch or want more deliberate digital arrangements; they may not know this service exists or how to name a problem. Departure: understand a specific practical gain, what the service does and the appropriate next decision. Tone: recognition, confidence and informed control; no assumed compromise, absolute safety or manufactured urgency. CTA: a private fit conversation, distinct from paid assessment and full hardening.

| Page / single objective | Core argument and reader gain | Proof / premium signal | Structure and density decisions |
|---|---|---|---|
| Why Toza, S2/C2: explain founder rationale | Personal expert judgment makes inherited digital arrangements understandable and deliberately configured | Founder learns situation and does work; client chooses changes and learns resulting setup | KEEP personal note/defaults insight and source-backed experience; REORGANIZE lower section by client gains; SHORTEN repeated founder delivery; LINK category detail to FAQ, process and prices |
| Pricing, S2/C2: explain what each purchase achieves | The first visit supplies understanding/action/next steps; full service supplies tailored hardening and ability to use it | Exact inclusions, scope, prices, keys, tests, record and support | PUT benefit before intake vocabulary; KEEP commercial tables and material boundaries; CLARIFY settings/identities/data/practices/training; KEEP visible optional follow-up |
| FAQ, S2/C2: resolve fit and informed-choice questions | Client can distinguish this intervention from routine support, software or monitoring and choose appropriately | Plain mechanisms, neutral comparisons, current operational answers | MOVE category question to opening fit section; MERGE strongest standalone insight/comparison; ADD focused service-model questions; KEEP all source question coverage and commercial/privacy details; avoid a second process catalog |
| How Toza helps, S3/C2: explain gain through the journey | Client moves from uncertainty to prioritized action, then separately to hardened arrangements they understand how to use | Approved urgent changes; explained sequencing; full-service settings/practices work, testing and teaching | REWRITE hero/metadata and headings around gains; KEEP qualification/purchase/stage boundaries; split implementation and teaching for visibility; LINK product detail to Pricing |
| Shared Copy, component review: preserve benefits and facts across reusable surfaces | A reader recognizes both the reason to contact and the intervention's value in short descriptions | Named expert, in-person work, defined stages and exclusions | REMOVE redundant contact eyebrow; apply approved low-knowledge heading; SHORTEN short service descriptions while linking full scope; add reusable service-model block; align nav/structured/reference facts and merged FAQ destination |

Required local boundaries: client authorization/presence/credential control; approval for material changes; qualification is not diagnosis; full hardening separate; no monitoring/security software; migration/data limits where relevant; no investigation/forensics/evidence/guarantee. Essential commercial facts stay exact; group-specific inclusions and support cannot become generic Bespoke promises.

Emotional movement: reduce the burden of knowing what to ask; explain what the client will understand, what changes and what they learn. Prestige follows judgment, direct delivery and verified work. No technical glossary, luxury adjectives, repeated persuasion or invented monitoring/coaching programme.

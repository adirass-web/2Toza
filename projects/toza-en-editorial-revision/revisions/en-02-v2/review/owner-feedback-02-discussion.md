No page deletion, redirect, navigation removal or canonical-architecture change is made.

Recommend retaining Personal Shield and Inner Circle Shield with nearby personal-cybersecurity/hardening-and-training descriptors. A software or insurance association is an editorial concern, not tested audience evidence. Names are not changed.

The only explicit no-monitoring wording appears in FAQ's adjustment-support answer. No security-software-installation exclusion is stated in the five candidates. The owner's D-014 clarification is therefore materially clearer than the current copy and should guide the next controlled revision.

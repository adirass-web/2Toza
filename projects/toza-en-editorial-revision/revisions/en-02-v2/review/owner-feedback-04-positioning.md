## Editor findings before rewriting

- Major / relevance and sales / full-service bridges: “if justified” makes the proposition depend on a fault. Replace with a positive result and a separate purchase boundary. Owner direction supplies authority.
- Major / proof and prestige / mechanism lists: familiar isolated examples can sound like the entire service. Explain interaction, consequences and expert work on the actual setup; retain limited recognition examples. No exclusive-knowledge claim.
- Moderate / clarity / Assessment duration: repeat of minimum and typical duration is longer than the owner's intended public signal. Apply D-020 consistently; retain outcome-based completion and no automatic cutoff in FAQ.
- Major / credibility / summary wording: removing conditional sales copy must not predetermine findings. Preserve open priorities and recommended next steps, including the choice to buy nothing further.
- Moderate / restraint / cross-page copy: avoid universal insecurity, inevitability of unauthorized access or accusation against a partner/relative. Keep existing authority/consent/non-forensic limits.

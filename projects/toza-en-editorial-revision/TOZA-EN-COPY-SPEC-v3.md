## 5. Scope, privacy and deliverables

Work stays within the authorized environment and agreed scope, using what is disclosed, observable or otherwise lawfully accessible. Material changes require approval; expanded work or price requires agreement first. No promise to discover every account or remove every exposure.

The client remains present and controls credentials. Toza does not keep credentials for ongoing use, remotely control or monitor devices, or install security software. Confidential handling is not invisible contact, invisible changes, no administrative records or no cloud systems. Keep the accepted qualified dataset/cloud-analysis and ordinary-lawful-business-record wording.

Essential migration means identity, recovery and critical-service access where needed, not historical archives or bulk data migration without separate scope. No investigation of another person, forensic reconstruction, evidence service or legal advice. A private dated record is a record of work, not certification, forensic evidence or a security guarantee. A blocked or declined action is not documented as completed. Attorney referral does not itself authorize technical access or disclosure.

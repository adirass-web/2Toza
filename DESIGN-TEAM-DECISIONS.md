# Toza — Study G: design team decisions (light version demo)

19 Oct 2026 · Team: art director, motion designer, colour & type designer, conversion & perception designer · Lead decision: Claude (owner delegated the choices, 15:44) · Inputs: spec v0.5, `claude/REFERENCES-study-g.md`, two rounds (round1-*.md, round2-*.md).

**Concept.** A white page ruled like a tailor's cutting table: a fine grid, a few boxes laid on it with deliberate empty cells, and the four situations let out one by one until the last fills the screen, the way a careful person opens a closed door.

## Decisions

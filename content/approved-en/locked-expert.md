# Why I created Toza.

You expect the best. You hold your advisers and yourself to a high standard. Yet your digital life probably still runs on settings someone else chose. I created Toza to give that part of your life the expert attention it deserves.

Dr. Lior Tabansky, founder of Toza

## A personal note

In 20 years of work in cyber defense, privacy and information security, I kept noticing a contradiction. People are discerning about their advisers, investments and personal choices, yet their phones, accounts and apps still run on defaults or arrangements someone made for them years ago.

Default settings don’t fit your circumstances. They can’t tell you what needs protection, what someone could piece together from details scattered across the web, or who can still see your information after your life changes. Anyone looking for an advantage against you already knows where to look.

I created Toza to bring my cybersecurity experience directly to discerning individuals: understand their circumstances, identify the threats and carry out protection fitted to their lives.

## Hands-on experience around the world {#experience}

At Toza, I apply that global experience and nuanced judgment to your private circumstances and personally carry out the agreed technical work. The expert who understands the situation is the expert making the changes with you.

## How that judgment becomes practical work.

The personal threat model sets the priorities for agreed changes to accounts, devices and everyday information practices. I work with you in person, explain material changes before you approve them, test the work and teach you how to use the resulting setup.

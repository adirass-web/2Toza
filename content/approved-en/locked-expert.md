# Why I created Toza.

People who give important decisions considerable care still entrust their digital lives to vendor defaults. I created Toza to give that part of your life the expert attention it deserves.

Dr. Lior Tabansky, founder of Toza

## A personal note

In two decades of cybersecurity work, I kept noticing a contradiction. People are discerning about their advisers, investments and personal choices, yet their phones, accounts and apps still run on defaults or arrangements someone made for them years ago.

Those settings were not chosen around their lives. They do not amount to a personal assessment of what needs protection, what bits of data across the web can reveal or how life changes affect access. Threat actors have noticed.

I created Toza to bring my cybersecurity experience directly to discerning individuals: understand their circumstances, identify the threats and carry out protection fitted to their lives.

**Dr. Lior Tabansky**

## Practical international experience {#experience}

My work has included national cybersecurity strategy and cyber-resilience projects with the World Bank across 11 countries, working with colleagues and local teams. My work involves a deep understanding of threat landscapes and rapid technological change, with complete confidentiality. I solve problems by setting priorities and turning recommendations into work that fits the people and systems involved.

At Toza, I apply that global experience and nuanced judgment to your private circumstances and personally carry out the agreed technical work. The expert who understands the situation is the expert making the changes with you.

## How that judgment becomes practical work.

The personal threat model sets the priorities for agreed changes to accounts, devices and everyday information practices. I work with you in person, explain material changes before you approve them, test the work and teach you how to use the resulting setup.

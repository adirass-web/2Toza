# Establish the risks. Build the protection around them.

Toza works with you in person, from understanding your circumstances to making and testing the agreed changes. Dr. Lior Tabansky personally carries out the work.

## First, your personal threat model.

We begin with your circumstances and the information you need to protect. Toza examines relevant apps, devices, accounts, access arrangements and digital traces to develop your personal threat model: the ways information could become exposed and the consequences that matter to you.

You receive a specific risk assessment, priorities and ordered next steps in the practical summary. This sets the plan for hardening, new accounts where needed, recovery and operational changes.

If we identify urgent exposure, Toza will address what can safely be fixed within the visit's agreed scope, with your approval. The visit completes the agreed intake, mapping, diagnosis and approved urgent first aid; you receive the summary whether or not you continue.

The assessment builds a comprehensive personal threat model within the agreed scope. The visit costs ₪4,000 and takes about half a day. It covers one main phone and one computer, iPad or second phone on the same main Apple or Android account.

## Then, separately agreed protection.

**Personal Shield** by Toza will harden the agreed setup, establish new core accounts and migrate essential access where needed, configure physical security keys, test material changes and teach you how to use the resulting arrangements. You receive a private dated record.

You remain present, control your credentials and approve material changes.

## Technical hardening and everyday practices.

The work considers both access and what information reveals: which accounts and devices need stronger protection, what digital traces disclose, and how separate details can reveal something consequential when combined. Operational security means changing the practices that expose information in daily life, alongside the technical settings.

## Protection you can use day to day.

The work addresses the fundamental access, sign-in, recovery and sharing arrangements to mitigate most of the risks and threat vectors. Testing and practical teaching help you use the changed setup in daily life.

Every protection tier includes a follow-up visit and a 60-day question line from completion of the protection work. Questions about the completed work are answered through WhatsApp or Signal within one working day. New work is agreed and priced separately.

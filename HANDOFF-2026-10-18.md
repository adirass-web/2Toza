## This session: the geometry search

1. **G3 mosaic (D38).** Rejected: too many right angles, too static.
2. **One 7° diagonal across the page (D39).** Rejected: still symmetrical.
3. **Studies A–E (D44).** Rejected: every shape still had parallel or mirrored sides. The five studies were:
   - A, crossing diagonals;
   - B, shards and overlap;
   - C, converging perspective;
   - D, staircase;
   - E, tilted plane.
4. **Clarifying the request.** The owner asked for "asymmetric motion-like shapes and bleed". Claude restated it as five rules, with a sketch:
   - no edges parallel;
   - every shape different;
   - bleed off the viewport and across sections;
   - cuts leaning one way;
   - text level.

   The owner said: build a single study, F.
5. **Study F (D45).** Built to those rules, and checked at 1024, 1440, 1920 and 390 px. It had:
   - irregular 4–5-point cuts;
   - an irregular grey field behind the photos;
   - photos bleeding off both edges;
   - the mint note crossing into the next section.

   **Owner verdict: "F is too much."** (D46)

**What this tells the next session.** The target lies between E and F. The owner wants:
- shapes that are not parallel or mirrored;
- a sense of motion and bleed;

but at less intensity than F. F changed several things at once: every photo got an irregular cut, a background field was added, and things bled on both sides and across the section. The owner has not said which of these was too much.

**Do not guess the next step.** Wait for the reference sites, then name the specific moves they share before proposing anything. The moves to look for: the number of cut corners, how many shapes are cut, how much bleed, and whether there is a background field.

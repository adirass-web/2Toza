## 2. Pricing decisions (main topic)

**Business goal:** higher margin at 1–2 engagements a month. Today one Personal Shield earns about ₪396 net per hour once the Assessment and travel are counted. The proposal roughly doubles the monthly result per engagement.

| # | Decision | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | **New price grid, to validate before launch:** first step **₪4,000** · Personal Shield **₪32,000** (1 person) · Personal Shield for Two **₪45,000** · Inner Circle **₪70,000** (up to 4) · larger households **from ₪90,000** | Approve for validation. Pre-approve the fallback grid: ₪3,600 / ₪28,000 / ₪40,000 / ₪60,000. |
| P2 | **The first step counts in full** toward a tier started within 30 days. After day 30, the tier is charged at its full price. | Approve |
| P3 | **Home network included in Personal Shield**; the two-person tier described as "for you and one other person" | Approve |
| P4 | **Inclusions in every tier:** up to three devices per person; a follow-up visit after about four weeks; questions answered by WhatsApp or Signal within one working day for 60 days (questions only, not new work, not emergencies); coordination with the client's lawyer or family office if authorised. Inner Circle adds a review visit at twelve months. | Approve |
| P5 | **Annual Care for past clients:** ₪12,000 (1 person) / ₪15,000 (Two) / ₪20,000 (household) a year. Includes the question line for 12 months, one check visit, new-device setup, a replacement key and a record update. Join within 6 months, otherwise start with a new first step. Internal cap of 8 plans, never stated. | Approve |
| P6 | **Attorney referral fees:** ₪600 on a paid first step (already set by Arnulf), then ₪2,000 / ₪3,000 / ₪4,500 when the client continues, and 5% for larger cases | Approve the continuation fees |
| P7 | **Validation:** 8–12 short interviews (clients and attorneys); quote the new prices to the next 5 enquiries; log the hours of the first 3 engagements. Launch if at least 40% would buy at ₪32,000; use the fallback grid below 25%. | Approve |

**Already set by Arnulf, for your confirmation:**
- No public sales, discounts or time-limited deals; the 30-day window is the only time element.
- Urgent surcharge unchanged (₪4,800 / ₪6,000 at weekends and holidays).
- Travel is not described on the site.
- Prices are shown without a VAT label (they remain VAT-inclusive).

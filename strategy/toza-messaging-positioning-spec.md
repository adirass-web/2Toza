# Toza messaging and positioning spec

19 November 2026 · Proposed editorial standard for Home, situation pages, paid landing pages, ads and referrals

## Positioning decision

**Toza designs, carries out and verifies private digital protection for people whose personal information can materially affect their money, reputation, relationships or freedom to decide.** The work is tailored to what the client needs to protect, who could use it, how it might become exposed and which parts of daily life still need to work.

The core buyer has consequential private affairs and has had little reason to examine digital exposure as a whole. They may already pay close attention to whom they trust, how they conduct a negotiation or when they share a personal decision. Their accounts, devices and everyday sharing may still be set up for older circumstances. Reach them **before a known incident**, while they have room to change the arrangements on their own terms. A private relationship, financial plan, family matter or transaction can carry that weight. Wealth, public profile and a live dispute are possible context, not qualification criteria.

**Primary product · Personal Shield and the other tailored protection engagements.** The protection is designed for the client and carried out in person. The Private Exposure Assessment is the paid first step that establishes a personal threat model and the priorities for protection. The assessment also stands on its own, with a written exposure summary and approved urgent first aid where safe and within scope. Closing one old sign-in makes the work tangible. The premium value is designing the whole agreed arrangement, changing it in an order that preserves essential access, and testing the changes with the client. ## The message sequence

1. **What’s at stake.** Name the private thing or decision. A deal can be affected if the other side learns whom the client is consulting. A move can become known before someone has decided what to tell their family. A reader should see themselves here without being assigned a crisis.
2. **Why it can already be exposed.** Explain one mechanism on a landing page and all three on the Exposure page. Access can outlast its purpose. Copies can already sit elsewhere. Separately harmless traces can reveal something consequential when combined. This is why waiting for a warning can cost the client room to act.
3. **What Toza builds.** Start with the client’s circumstances and map what needs protection, from whom, and what depends on the existing setup. Agree the scope, then harden the relevant accounts, devices, sign-in, recovery and sharing. Where the scope calls for it, establish new core accounts, move essential access, configure security keys and change everyday practices. End with testing, teaching and a private dated record. Show one meaningful change as an example.
4. **Why the work merits a premium.** One senior expert carries the judgment through implementation and verification. Toza handles interdependent changes with the client present, in control of their credentials and approving material changes. Explain the written scope, the work that will be tested, the record the client receives and the included follow-up for the protection tier. Use “tailored” only where the next sentence shows what is fitted to the client. [Approved approach](../content/approved-en/locked-approach.md)
5. **How to begin.** Keep **Start with a free private conversation**, with WhatsApp and Signal. Explain the conversation, assessment and paid protection sequence once per LP. If the CTA repeats, repeat the action and use the closing placement for approved first-message privacy guidance. Use Pricing and Conditions for the full terms. The published assessment is ₪4,000. Personal Shield for one person is ₪32,000. The assessment is credited in full when protection work begins within 30 days of assessment completion. The credit is a commercial term. ### Urgency and claim boundaries

**Use the period before the information matters.** “Before you start negotiating, consider what the other side could already learn from the accounts you use.” “Before you share a plan, find out who can already see parts of it.” The deadline comes from a real move, meeting, handover, filing or deal. In a proactive case, the reason to start is the freedom to make considered changes before a deadline dictates the order. Once someone else holds a copy, changing access cannot retrieve it. Use that limit to explain the value of early action. [Exposure](../content/approved-en/locked-exposure.md) · [Service limits](../src/content/en/faq.md)

Describe outcomes within the agreed, authorized scope. “Toza hardens the agreed setup and tests material changes” is supportable. Claims of total protection, invisible changes, guaranteed removal, past attribution, or a result only Toza can deliver require evidence the current service does not provide. Present credentials as evidence of judgment and distinguish them from endorsement. Use clearly labelled illustrative material for a sample record. Confirm any new example or claim with the service owner before publication.

## Debate and review plan

### Independent reviews applied to this draft

Two AI reviewers independently read the source material and argued from premium positioning and skeptical-buyer/claims perspectives. Their reviews informed the draft. Human validation is a separate checkpoint.

| Direction challenged | What the reviewers argued | Decision in this draft |
| --- | --- | --- |
| People with “big things to hide” | As public wording, it imputes wrongdoing and can repel someone with legitimate confidential affairs. | **Revise.** Keep the audience bar high. Name the money, relationships, reputation and decisions at stake. |
| People are exposed unless they harden with Toza | Clients may already have good protections. The visibility gap is credible, while exclusive effectiveness and certain exposure are unproven. | **Revise.** Explain the exposure paths clients may overlook. Differentiate through the work Toza undertakes and verifies. |
| Urgency should feel stronger | A countdown can sound manipulative. An approaching disclosure or decision can create real stakes, and existing copies give the timing real consequences. | **Keep the urgency, change the reason.** Specify what is coming up and what the client stands to lose control over. |
| Lead with “Toza fixes” | Individual fixes make the service legible, but a small task as the main proposition makes the premium hard to understand. | **Reject as the lead.** Lead with designing and carrying out protection. Use fixes as evidence. |
| Foreground the founder to prove value | A named expert increases confidence. Repeated at every entry point, it conflicts with older voice direction and distracts from the work. | **Revise by surface.** Use the owner-approved credential placement for proof. Make the service and client stakes do the work in LP openings. |

### Required checkpoints for future messaging work

1. **Challenge the brief before copy freezes.** Assign an independent positioning reviewer to argue what could make the proposed direction sound generic, accusatory or cheap. Assign a service-delivery reviewer to challenge every material claim and a conversion reviewer to challenge the reader’s path to contact. Require each to offer one stronger alternative, including the tradeoff.
2. **Bring in human expertise before publication.** Have Dr. Tabansky verify the named work and boundaries. Seek independent review from a premium-services copy strategist and an adviser who hears these concerns, such as a family or commercial attorney. Have a paid-search specialist review the actual keywords, copy, landing pages and measurement plan before spend. Seek these reviews through the project owner. AI critiques alone do not close this checkpoint.
3. **Record disagreements explicitly.** For each contested direction, record the original line or decision, reviewer objection, stronger alternative, evidence, and owner disposition of **keep, revise or reject** with a reason. Carry unresolved claims forward as open decisions, including when the objection is to the owner’s preferred direction.
4. **Test comprehension before optimizing conversion.** Show the Home and one situation opening to prospective clients or referrers without describing the service in advance. Ask what Toza actually does, who it is for, whether a known incident is required, and what justifies the price. Revise if readers consistently describe a settings fix, an investigation, or an assessment-only service. Compare qualified conversations and paid protection uptake when there is enough volume.
5. **Repeat at each positioning change and paid launch.** Revisit the debate log if a new audience, stronger urgency line or materially different service claim is introduced. Handoff order is Home and Separation examples, other situation pages, then paid variants. Use the approved spec as the review brief for each.

## Website: /en/separation-divorce/

**Some things should be yours to tell.**

You have learned something that may change your relationship. Before deciding what to do about a possible separation, you arrange a viewing or a meeting with an adviser. A shared calendar could reveal those plans before you choose whom to tell.

Toza designs, carries out and tests digital protection in person, around your plans and the accounts and devices you rely on.

**Start with a free private conversation**
WhatsApp · Signal

### Protection built around your next steps

Toza maps what needs protection, from whom, and what must keep working. You agree a written scope, then one senior expert hardens the relevant accounts, devices and sharing with you in person, in an order that preserves essential access. Together you test the material changes.

For example, an old device may still be signed in to an account. Within the agreed scope, Toza can help close access you are authorized to manage, after explaining possible interruptions or notifications and checking what depends on it. You stay in control of your credentials and approve material changes. Changing access cannot retrieve a copy someone already holds.

### How to begin

Start with a free private conversation, usually 10–20 minutes. The Private Exposure Assessment is a separate paid first step: about half a day in person for ₪4,000. It identifies what needs protection, from whom, and the priorities for action within the agreed scope, provides a written exposure summary and includes approved urgent first aid where safe and within scope.

Protection work is agreed separately. Personal Shield for one person is ₪32,000 in total. The assessment fee is credited in full when protection work begins within 30 days of assessment completion. Protection includes a private dated record, a follow-up visit and a 60-day question line; see Pricing for the scope and terms. Toza can coordinate timing with your attorney if you authorize it.

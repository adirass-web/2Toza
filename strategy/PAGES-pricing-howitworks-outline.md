# Pricing and How it works: two pages, two jobs (outline for approval, 18 Oct 2026)

Owner feedback: "The pricing & how-it-works pages are very similar and none is good."

## Diagnosis
- Both pages carry the same first-step card, the same three tiers and the same three stages. How it works ends as a second Pricing page.
- Neither answers its own question well. How it works doesn't show what the client experiences or why the method is different. Pricing doesn't make the choice between tiers easy.

## The rule
| Page | Visitor's question | It owns | It does not show |
|---|---|---|---|
| **How it works** | "What actually happens, and why should I trust the method?" | The method, the experience, what you keep, how you stay in control | Price cards or tier lists (one line and a link to Fees only) |
| **Pricing (Fees)** | "Which one is for me, and what exactly do I pay?" | The comparison, the credit rule, larger households, Annual Care, the terms link | Stage descriptions or drawings |

## How it works: new outline
1. **Hero (typography only):** headline about the method, not the person; one-line lead; actions.
2. **The method, stated plainly:** Harden is Toza's own method, developed from public guidance of government security agencies and security vendors. Two or three short proof points on *how* it works, such as the work being done in person with you present, nothing changing without your approval, and every change being explained and tested. Facts only, from existing copy.
3. **The stages:** the new section (option A, B or C, which you choose). It carries stage, outcome and what you keep, and replaces today's three blocks.
4. **What it is like on the day:** where it happens (home or office), how long the first step takes (half a day), what to have ready, and that you keep control of your passwords. Reuses existing FAQ facts; no new claims.
5. **What you keep:** the sample exposure summary document (moved from Home or shared with it), plus the dated record.
6. **After the work:** follow-up visit after about four weeks, the 60-day question line, then Annual Care. Three lines with a link to Fees.
7. **One line on price:** "The first step is ₪4,000 and counts toward protection within 30 days. Fees →"
8. **Contact.**

Removed from How it works: the offer card, the tier list and the "senior check-up" price block, all of which now live only on Pricing.

## Pricing (Fees): new outline
1. **Hero (typography only):** "Fees", one-line lead: fixed prices agreed in writing before work.
2. **First step, alone:** a single row, not a card. First step ₪4,000, half a day, written summary yours to keep, and it counts in full toward a tier started within 30 days.
3. **Comparison table:** one table with three columns (Personal Shield ₪32,000 · for Two ₪45,000 · Inner Circle ₪70,000) and the same rows for each:
   - Who it covers (1 person · you and one other · household of up to 4)
   - Everyday devices (up to 3 per person)
   - Home network (included)
   - Security keys (2 · 4 · 8)
   - Follow-up visit (after about four weeks)
   - Question line (60 days)
   - Review visit at twelve months (Inner Circle)

   On phones it becomes one tier at a time, with the rows kept. It is easier to compare than three cards and visibly calmer.
4. **Larger households:** one line, "Several residences, staff or more people: one fixed price after the first step, from ₪90,000."
5. **After the 60 days: Annual Care:** three prices in one line (₪12,000 · ₪15,000 · ₪20,000 a year) and what it includes.
6. **How payment works:** written offer, acceptance and payment before work, with no sales or time-limited deals (one or two sentences from Conditions).
7. **All terms → Conditions.**
8. **Contact.**

Removed from Pricing: the Assess–Harden–Verify price strip (stages belong to How it works) and the method paragraph (moved to How it works).

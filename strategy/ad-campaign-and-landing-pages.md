# Toza search campaign and landing-page plan

> Campaign planning reference. The [messaging spec](toza-messaging-positioning-spec.md) supersedes this draft’s fixes-first positioning and governs all new copy.

19 November 2026 · Strategy and draft copy for review

## 1. The proposition

**Toza fixes the ways someone can still get to information you meant to keep private.** Dr. Lior Tabansky makes agreed changes with you, in person. Depending on what he finds, that can mean closing an old sign-in, correcting who can recover an account or changing what is shared. Toza addresses urgent exposure that can safely be fixed during the paid assessment, within the agreed scope and with your approval. Broader work, including setting up new core accounts, migrating essential access, configuring security keys and testing changes, is agreed separately. Use the examples only where Toza confirms it can deliver them for the relevant situation. [Approved Toza approach](../content/approved-en/locked-approach.md)

The conversion is a qualified request for a free private conversation. The conversation usually takes 10–20 minutes. Keep the existing CTA **Start with a free private conversation**, followed by WhatsApp and Signal. Use the existing neutral first message, and keep the safe-contact guidance within reach of the first click. The paid assessment costs ₪4,000, takes about half a day in person, and leaves the client with a written exposure summary. Include the credit terms once on the website or through Pricing. ### What to take from the four drafts

| Reference | Strongest idea to keep | How it should change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Divorce | Location and private messages matter before anyone has filed or moved out. | Start as early as discovering a partner’s affair. Keep the focus on making one’s own plans privately. The draft’s assertion that a partner is already snooping claims more than Toza can know. |
| Business dispute | A leak can affect negotiations, client relationships and reputation. | Pick one believable access path, such as an old personal address that still recovers a business account. Offer fixes only for accounts and devices the client is authorized to change. |
| Commercial spying | A private phone or mailbox can expose deal information outside company systems. | Reframe as executive digital exposure or deal confidentiality. Treat it as a different reason to buy within Business initially. Drop the predictions about sabotage and stolen clients. |
| Inheritance conflict | A family arrangement from years ago can expose current correspondence. | Use a letter to counsel or a shared document before it circulates. Focus on the client’s own access and permissions. Claims about hidden assets pull the page into estate investigation. |

The drafts’ shared “every hour” line is easy to reuse and easy to stop believing. Use an actual upcoming event. Keep consequences plausible and conditional. Google also restricts sensationalism and ads that exploit negative life events to pressure immediate action. [Google clickbait policy](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/15936667)

The affair belongs in the **website** story as a turning point. It can make the page feel earlier and more human. Headlining it in paid search would pull in queries about proving infidelity or monitoring a partner, which are a different service. Write ads about protecting the client’s own access. Google restricts personalized targeting for relationship hardships and prohibits ads for tools or services promoted for unauthorized monitoring. Current policy does not amount to preapproval of this campaign. [Google relationship-hardship policy](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/16700849) · [Google unauthorized surveillance policy](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/6016086)

### What Toza fixes on each page

- **Separation.** Close an outdated sign-in where appropriate, put recovery back under the client’s control and adjust sharing around travel, meetings and correspondence. Sequence changes around access the client still relies on. Use the same examples on the paid page, cut to the fixes most relevant to the ad.
- **Business dispute.** Replace a personal recovery address on an authorized business account, separate private and work access where they overlap, and verify critical services still work after the change. When system ownership is disputed, limit the offer to access the client can authorize.
- **Commercial exposure.** Separate deal correspondence and sensitive files from shared personal access. As separately agreed work, Toza can move essential access to a protected account, strengthen sign-in and test the new arrangement.
- **Inheritance.** Change who can recover the client’s account, retire unnecessary signed-in devices and adjust sharing on the client’s own folders or correspondence. Keep estate assets and third-party accounts outside this service promise.

## 4. Paid media pilot

1. **Start with Google Search.** Test two small ad groups with separately visible spend where budget allows. Start with Separation personal privacy and Business account separation. Use search intent and the service area, with no remarketing, customer lists, lookalikes or audience expansion. Choose presence in the confirmed in-person service area. Use English first if that matches verified page and intake coverage. Add Hebrew only once the Hebrew ad, destination and intake are ready. [Google location options](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/1722038) · [Google sensitive targeting](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/143465)
2. **Validate searches before spending.** Run Keyword Planner in the confirmed market. Try exact and phrase match for terms such as “digital privacy during separation,” “personal digital security consultation,” “separate personal and business accounts,” and “secure personal accounts business dispute.” Search terms like “spy on partner,” “catch cheating spouse,” “hack someone’s phone,” “private investigator,” “divorce lawyer” and “probate forms” belong in the negative-keyword review. Use narrow match types for negatives so legitimate protective searches still qualify. Review actual search terms daily in the pilot. [Google keyword and landing-page guidance](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6238826)
3. **Prepare two responsive search ad concepts per group.** One leads with a tangible fix. The other leads with a decision or handover coming up. Run concepts sequentially if volume is low. Build distinct copy combinations that read coherently in any order. Format limits are 30 characters per headline and 90 per description. Add approved Pricing, How Toza Works and About sitelinks where they support the same intent. Review automated text and URL settings so the pilot uses the intended creative and destination. [Google responsive search ad guidance](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7684791)
4. **Expand after business evidence.** Test the executive exposure group only if the business queries or adviser referrals show distinct needs. Give inheritance a paid test only when Keyword Planner or actual search terms justify it. Use adviser referrals to the website pages throughout. If a social test makes sense later, start with broad creative about keeping personal accounts private. Avoid copy that presumes a sensitive personal situation and keep the pilot free of Meta Pixel and Conversions API. [Meta ad standards](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/objectionable-content/privacy-violations-personal-attributes/) · [Meta Business Tools terms](https://www.facebook.com/legal/terms/businesstools)

## 5. Spend, measurement and review

- **Budget from scope and margin.** In Keyword Planner, forecast the cost and probable volume in the confirmed service area. Set an owner-approved spend ceiling and budget for a two-week pilot. Derive a maximum cost per qualified inquiry from the contribution margin per paid assessment, the observed share of qualified inquiries that book, and the portion of margin the business can reinvest. Start with assessment economics. Add expected protection margin once there is enough evidence. No forecast or spend assumed here.
- **Define qualification before launch.** A qualified inquiry is about the client’s own or authorized access, fits the in-person service area and scope, and can progress to a private conversation. Record aggregate paid visits, messaging opens, qualified enquiries, conversations held and paid assessments. Count messaging app handoffs and completed conversations separately. The primary business indicators are cost per qualified inquiry and cost per paid assessment, when attribution supports them.
- **Keep measurement proportionate.** Use an aggregate, first-party count for messaging opens with an opaque campaign code. Keep inquiry notes separate from analytics. Ask an optional “How did you hear about Toza?” during intake. Report uncertain attribution as unknown. Avoid customer uploads, enhanced conversions, sensitive URLs and query text in third-party events, session replay, remarketing tags and any transfer of names, message text or private circumstances. Google expressly lists divorce and marital separation under restrictions on enhanced conversion and store-sales measurement. Generic event labels alone do not remove sensitivity. [Google customer data policy](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/7475709) · [Meta Business Tools terms](https://www.facebook.com/legal/terms/businesstools)
- **Make weekly decisions from qualified demand.** Pause clearly irrelevant or unsafe terms as they appear. After the first week, compare the two groups against the inquiry-cost ceiling and review whether enquiries are within scope. Decide what to retain after week two. If the pilot is too small to judge, report what the search terms and intake showed. Compare one change at a time, starting with the ad’s concrete fix or the lead’s timing. Reserve spend for a second test only where the first search terms were relevant.

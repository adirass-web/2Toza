## 1. Recommended architecture

**The core constraint.** One expert, 80 hours a month on paper, about 60–65 real hours after sales and admin. The plan is **one engagement a month, plus one or two first steps, plus Care**; two engagements a month fit only for two Personal Shields, and only just. So every price must work at **one engagement a month**, and every inclusion is paid for in the price. Value metric stays **people protected** (P3); hours never appear on the site.

**Personal Shield at ₪32,000, not ₪30,000.** The home network and router are included for one person (P7z): the separating spouse living alone, often in a home whose router the other side set up, needs the network most. At ₪30,000 with network and the larger device allowance, Shield earns ₪598/h (₪515 buffered); at ₪32,000, ₪640/h (₪552). **Recommendation: ₪32,000** (§2): it keeps the main tier clearly above the ₪600/h yardstick (P1).

**Two at ₪45,000 (1.41× Shield)** sits below P5 #5's "above 1.5×". Deliberate: a second place for ₪13,000 makes the two-person option plainly right for pairs (the typical household, P4 #11), and still earns ₪720/h on its 14 extra hours.

**What the buyer pays (first step folded in)** **[Owner-approved 16 Oct]**. Tier prices include the first step; tier totals are unchanged by the lower first-step price. The client pays ₪4,000 first; if a tier starts within **30 days**, the ₪4,000 counts in full and the client pays the balance (₪28,000 / ₪41,000 / ₪66,000). Conditions: after day 30, the tier is charged at its full price; if the first step shows the next tier is needed, the client pays that tier's price less the ₪4,000 already paid, within the same 30 days. Partial credit rejected: it raises the total while looking like a penalty.

**Why ₪4,000 works as the first step.** It is framed in the health-check format, "a senior check-up of your digital life": half a day in person with Dr. Tabansky, urgent first aid on the spot, and a written exposure summary you keep. The lead message is that it **counts in full toward your protection if you continue within 30 days**. No health-check price is named and no per-hour comparison appears in copy. It stays easy to refer and within reach of a spouse with frozen funds (P7z §3); tier totals carry the margin.

## 3. Why these numbers

**Anchors (P5).** ₪32,000 ≈ two days of a two-person executive protection team ($3,000–5,000/day). The first step borrows the executive health-check format (P5 #2) without quoting its price. Never priced against IT or freelancer rates (P1); no hours or per-hour comparisons shown.

**Price as signal.** ₪14,000 can read as a technician's fee; ₪32,000 sits in the same mental account as the buyer's lawyer. Premium needs a story (P5 #8): one named expert, in person, his own method.

**Psychology applied.**
- *Anchoring and order:* Inner Circle and the "from ₪90,000" line are in view; ₪32,000 is judged against ₪70,000, not zero.
- *Low-friction first step:* ₪4,000 that counts in full is a small, safe commitment (foot-in-the-door) that leads to the tier total already shown.
- *Per-person contrast:* "for Two" at ₪13,000 more makes the second place the obvious choice for pairs.
- *Risk reversal, premium form:* no refund guarantee; instead a stand-alone first step, scope and price fixed in writing, a follow-up visit, 60 days of answered questions.

**Israeli context.** Lawyer-led word of mouth: one public price, no negotiation, no public promotions (P5 #7); published prices filter price-shoppers.

**Risk of too high.** Separating spouses with frozen funds (1b P1) drop out; attorneys hesitate. Mitigation: the ₪4,000 first step visibly stands alone and handles urgent first aid; fallback grid ready (§5).
**Risk of too low.** At ₪28,000 or less with these inclusions, Shield falls to ~₪556/h and the extras eat the gain.

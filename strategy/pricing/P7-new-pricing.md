## 1. Recommended architecture

**The core constraint.** One expert, 80 hours a month on paper, about 60–65 real hours after sales and admin. The plan is **one engagement a month, plus one or two first steps, plus Care**; two engagements a month fit only for two Personal Shields, and only just. So every price must work at **one engagement a month**, and every inclusion is paid for in the price. Value metric stays **people protected** (P3); hours never appear on the site.

**Personal Shield at ₪32,000, not ₪30,000.** The home network and router are now included for one person (P7z): the separating spouse living alone, often in a home whose router the other side set up, needs the network most. At ₪30,000 with network and the larger device allowance, Shield earns ₪583/h (₪502 buffered); at ₪32,000, ₪625/h (₪538). **Recommendation: ₪32,000** (§2). Both are round; ₪32,000 keeps the main tier above the ₪600/h yardstick (P1).

**Two at ₪45,000 (1.41× Shield)** sits below P5 #5's "above 1.5×". This is a deliberate choice: a second place for ₪13,000 makes the two-person option plainly right for couples and partners (the typical household, P4 #11), and still earns ₪720/h on its 14 extra hours.

**What the buyer pays (first step folded in).** Tier prices include the first step. Buyer-facing: "You pay ₪5,000 for the first step. If you continue within 30 days, you pay the balance of the fee." Conditions state: after day 30, the tier is charged at its full price (the ₪5,000 is not deducted); if the first step shows the next tier is needed, you pay that tier's price less the ₪5,000 already paid, within the same 30 days. Window 14 → 30 days **[Owner decision required]**: proactive buyers and couples consult each other. Partial credit rejected: it raises the total while looking like a penalty.

### Complex-household rule (no micro-fees)

> If the first step shows more than the tier includes (more people or devices, a second home, staff, company devices), Dr. Tabansky proposes the next tier, with its full allowance, before any work begins. Beyond Inner Circle, he sets one fixed price in writing. There are no per-device or per-hour charges.

No meter; a step-up brings more, so it reads as fit, not penalty. With the network in Shield, a single person moves up only for extra devices or a second home. The buffer absorbs ordinary variance; P5 #4 ("when unsure, quote the higher tier") governs edge cases.

## 3. Why these numbers

**Anchors (P5).** ₪32,000 ≈ two days of a two-person executive protection team ($3,000–5,000/day); the ₪5,000 first step sits below an executive physical ($2,700–4,000). Never priced against IT or freelancer rates (P1); no hours shown.

**Price as signal.** ₪14,000 can read as a technician's fee; ₪32,000 sits in the same mental account as the buyer's lawyer. Premium needs a story (P5 #8): one named expert, in person, his own method.

**Psychology applied.**
- *Anchoring and order:* Inner Circle and the "from ₪100,000" line are in view; ₪32,000 is judged against ₪70,000, not zero.
- *Per-person contrast:* "for Two" at ₪13,000 more makes the second place the obvious choice for pairs.
- *Round prices:* fear- and relief-led purchases convert better at round figures (P5 #9).
- *Risk reversal, premium form:* no refund guarantee; instead a stand-alone first step, scope and price fixed in writing, a follow-up visit, 90 days of answered questions.

**Israeli context.** Lawyer-led word of mouth: one public price, no negotiation (P5 #7); published prices filter price-shoppers.

**Risk of too high.** Separating spouses with frozen funds (1b P1) drop out; attorneys hesitate. Mitigation: the ₪5,000 first step visibly stands alone and handles urgent first aid; fallback grid ready (§5).
**Risk of too low.** At ₪28,000 or less with these inclusions, Shield falls to ~₪540/h and the extras eat the gain.

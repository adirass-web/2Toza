# P7z — Independent review of P7 new pricing (15 Oct 2026)

Panel: (a) pricing expert, (b) affluent buyer in a divorce or founder dispute, (c) referring family-law attorney. Inputs: P4–P6, BRIEF, decisions.

## Verdict

**Approve for validation, with changes.** The grid follows owner direction, and the core arithmetic is correct. Three things are weaker than presented: the Annual Care economics are overstated, "1–2 engagements a month" is really "one, plus first steps", and the network fence and the "for Two" wording work against the divorce buyer, the core persona.

## 1. Arithmetic (recomputed, P6 method)

Recomputed: first step ₪530/h (buffered 461), Shield 685 (593), Two 680 (585), Inner Circle 857 (740), larger ~878 (754), Care 725 (625) and 740 (631). All match P7. Care household net (₪11,102) implies 2 replacement keys; the text says one.

Monthly nets, "₪40k in 61 h" and "₪15,000 for 17 h" check. Fallback 591/600/726 ₪/h applies to ₪26k/40k/60k; ₪4,500 earns ₪477. Label it.

Errors in the inputs, not the maths:
- **Care hours.** The engagement table costs 90 days of direct line at 3–4 h. Care offers the line *all year* plus a visit, a key and a re-check after a new phone, in 8 hands-on hours. Consistently costed (line 12–16 h, visit 3, re-check 2, travel) it is 20–24 h: **₪300–360/h**, not ₪725 (cf. P6 §4).
- **Devices "absorbed by buffer".** The allowance goes up 50% (IC 8 → 12) with no hours added, which spends the buffer P6 kept for variance. At about 1.25 h per extra device: Shield ₪662/h, Two ₪649/h, IC ₪795/h. Still viable; say so.
- **Travel included across Israel.** The 2 h per visit figure is a Central Israel number. North or Negev visits take 4–5 h.
- **Two-person tier at 1.5×** goes against P5 #5 ("above 1.5×"). Defensible; state it as a choice.

## 2. Capacity

On 80 h with no admin, two Shields barely fit (72 h; 83 h buffered); two "for Two" or two Inner Circles do not. Uncounted overhead: non-converting first steps (8 h each), the Care cap (about 15 h on P7's figures, 20–25 h on realistic ones), Inner Circle twelve-month reviews landing in a later month, and three overlapping 90-day lines. With 60–65 real hours, the plan is **one engagement a month, plus one or two first steps, plus Care**. The "₪100k from" engagement (~90 h, ~105 h buffered) takes more than a full month.

## 3. Price levels

- **₪5,000 first step.** Right for (b), easy for (c).
- **₪14k → ₪30k (2.1×).** It works for founders and estates. It is hard for a separating spouse whose funds are frozen. The ₪5,000 first step must visibly stand alone for them.
- **Two at ₪45k.** The level is good (≈ P6 premium ₪46k). The issue is the framing (see §5).

## 4. First step included, 30-day window

Economics sound (c-bundled), reading unclear: "the first step is included" suggests a free extra, and day 31 and step-up after the Assessment are unstated. 30 days is right.

## 5. Inclusions

Strong: follow-up visit, lawyer coordination, twelve-month review, per-person privacy. Weak:
- **"Direct line for 90 days"** has no defined response time. Litigation buyers will read it as on call: an overpromise for one person.
- **Device counts as the first bullet** read like a phone plan. Name the devices instead of counting them.
- **Network only from Two.** The divorce buyer living alone, often in the former marital home where the ex set up the router, needs the network most. Under the fence, that person pays ₪45k for a "two people" tier.
- **"For two people sharing a home"** describes the spouse this buyer is separating from.

## 6. Complex-household rule and "from ₪100,000"

The no-meter rule is right. Its gap is that device or home overflow for one person is solved by a tier named for two people. The ₪70k → ₪100k cliff (+43%) for a fifth person invites under-scoping. (₪90k still earns ~₪900/h.) Keep the one line; the figure filters (P5 #10).

## 7. Attorney view

₪5,000 is easy to refer, and "coordinates with your lawyer" helps. At ₪30,000 the attorney needs one sentence of why, plus certainty. The referral fee (₪300–3,300) was set on old prices and must be rescaled before launch, not after.

## 8. Copy

The voice is right: "Fees", calm lead, method block, no badges, no hours. Keep "white-glove" (banned) out of copy. The Home block conflicts with V-007 (₪3,600 / ₪14,000) until a decision supersedes it.

## Required changes

1. Recost Annual Care at 20–24 h. Either define the line narrowly (a visit, a phone re-check, a key, questions by message) and keep ₪9,000, or price it at ₪12,000+. Fix the household key count.
2. Replace "Direct line for 90 days" with a defined form, e.g. "Questions answered by WhatsApp or Signal for 90 days, within one working day".
3. Include the home network in Personal Shield. At ₪30,000 that gives ₪602/h; at ₪32,000, ₪644/h. Alternatively let the step-up rule offer the network without the "Two" label.
4. Reword "for Two" as "For you and one other person" and drop "sharing a home".
5. Rewrite the window line: "You pay ₪5,000 for the first step. If you continue within 30 days, you pay the balance of the fee." Put day-31 terms and step-up terms in Conditions.
6. Travel: "Travel in Central Israel included; elsewhere in Israel by arrangement", or add travel hours to the costing.
7. Card bullets: name the devices instead of counting them; keep the caps in Conditions.
8. Restate capacity as "one engagement a month plus first steps", with the monthly net at 60–65 h. Add a device-hours line to §2.
9. Consider ₪90,000 for the "from" line.
10. Rescale the referral fee, and add an attorney one-pager with the fixed prices, before T2 live quotes.

## Persona reactions

**Pricing expert.** Disciplined: round, fixed, one metric, no meter. Care is a ₪300/h product dressed as ₪725, and the open direct line is a hidden capacity cost. Fix both and the grid holds at one engagement a month.

**Affluent buyer.** ₪30,000 next to my legal fees is serious, not shocking; fixed in writing is what I want. But I am not "two people sharing a home", and I need my router done because my husband set it up. Tell me what I owe if I need 40 days.

**Family-law attorney.** The ₪5,000 first step I refer without hesitation; ₪30,000 to liquid clients if I can say "fixed, in writing, he coordinates with me". For a client with frozen accounts I need to know the first step stands on its own. Settle the referral fee before I see the new prices.

## Owner decisions (prioritised)

1. Grid ₪5,000 / ₪30,000 / ₪45,000 / ₪70,000 for validation, fallback pre-approved.
2. Home network in Personal Shield (₪30k or ₪32k), and the "for Two" wording.
3. Annual Care: narrow inclusions at ₪9,000, or ₪12,000+.
4. Defined form of the 90-day line (channel and response time).
5. First step folded in, 30-day window, with the buyer-facing wording above.
6. Travel scope: Central Israel included or all of Israel included.
7. "From" figure (₪90k or ₪100k), and referral-fee rescale before live quotes.

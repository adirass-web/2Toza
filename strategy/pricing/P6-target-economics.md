# P6 Target economics: price needed for a higher margin at 1-2 engagements a month (15 Oct 2026)

Workbook with live formulas: `P6-target-economics.xlsx` (yellow cells on Inputs). All prices are VAT-inclusive (18%); "net" means after VAT and hardware. Everything below is an option or a calculation; no price is recommended. Target income was not given by the owner, so section 1 is a ladder, not a verdict.

## Assumptions (from P4; changes marked)

- Hours: Assessment 6, Shield 16, Inner Circle 32 (4 days x 8). Travel 2 h per visit.
- Visits: 1 person 3 (Assessment 1 + Shield 2); 2 people 4 (+1); 4 people 5 (Assessment 1 + Inner Circle 4, as in P4's 48 h).
- Hardware, net of VAT: 2 keys per person at ₪450 gross, router ₪700 gross, both assumed VAT-inclusive (₪381 and ₪593 net). Treated as cost with no input-VAT recovery beyond that.
- Two-person engagement: Assessment 6 + Shield 16 + extra person 10 = 32 h hands-on, 4 visits (8 h), 4 keys.
- Buffer: +20% on hands-on hours only (travel fixed), to test whether a fixed price survives a larger household. It is a stress case, not a planned allowance.
- Capacity 80 h/month, with no deduction for sales, admin, leave or sick days. Real capacity is lower.

## 1. Price required per engagement (2-person engagement, 40 h, hardware ₪1,525 net)

Gross price = (monthly target / engagements + 1,525) x 1.18. Net ₪/h uses 40 h; the figure in brackets uses 46.4 h (buffer).

| Monthly net target | 1 engagement/month | 1.5/month | 2/month |
|---|---|---|---|
| ₪40k | ₪49.0k gross, ₪1,000/h (862) | ₪33.3k, ₪667/h (575) | ₪25.4k, ₪500/h (431) |
| ₪60k | ₪72.6k, ₪1,500/h (1,293) | ₪49.0k, ₪1,000/h (862) | ₪37.2k, ₪750/h (647) |
| ₪80k | ₪96.2k, ₪2,000/h (1,724) | ₪64.7k, ₪1,333/h (1,149) | ₪49.0k, ₪1,000/h (862) |
| ₪100k | ₪119.8k, ₪2,500/h (2,155) | ₪80.5k, ₪1,667/h (1,437) | ₪60.8k, ₪1,250/h (1,078) |

Reading: today's two-person price (₪26,000) is ₪513/h; the P1 benchmark is about ₪600/h. Only ₪40k at 2 per month (₪25.4k) is near today's price; every other cell needs more.

**Utilisation and white-glove room (80 h capacity).** Spare hours per engagement = 80 / engagements - engagement hours.

| Engagement | Hours (buffered) | 1 per month: use / spare h | 1.5 per month | 2 per month |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 person | 28 (32.4) | 35% / 52 (47.6) | 52% / 25.3 (20.9) | 70% / 12.0 (7.6) |
| 2 people | 40 (46.4) | 50% / 40 (33.6) | 75% / 13.3 (6.9) | 100% / 0 (-6.4) |
| 4 people | 48 (55.6) | 60% / 32 (24.4) | 90% / 5.3 (-2.3) | 120% / -8 (-15.6) |

Utilisation is 35-100% on the owner's hours, not the brief's 25-60%, because a two-person job already takes 40 h. At 1 engagement/month there is room for about 30-40 extra hours per two-person engagement (for example an extra visit of 4 h, 2 follow-up visits, a month of after-hours availability). At 1.5/month only about 7-13 h. At 2/month of two-person engagements there is none, and two Inner Circles a month do not fit. Extras are only free of capacity cost at 1 engagement/month. Extra hours dilute net ₪/h: a two-person job at ₪36k (₪725/h) falls to ₪527/h if 15 h are added.

## 2. Engagement economics at the P4/P3 reference prices and with the 20% buffer

Reference prices (today's published/working, Assessment fully credited): 1 person ₪14,000; 2 people ₪26,000 (P3 working add-on ₪12,000); 4 people ₪42,000.

| Type | Net after hardware | Net ₪/h | Net ₪/h, +20% buffer | Price needed for ₪600/h at +20% buffer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 person | ₪11,102 | 396 | 343 | ₪23.8k (¹) |
| 2 people | ₪20,508 | 513 | 442 | ₪34.7k |
| 2 people, extra person 6 h / 16 h | ₪20,508 | 570 / 446 | 493 / 383 | n/a |
| 4 people | ₪31,949 | 666 | 575 | ₪43.7k |

(¹) (600 x 32.4 + 763) x 1.18. ₪600/h is a yardstick (P1), not a target.

Observations: a second person (10 h, one extra visit) raises ₪/h from 396 to 513 versus one person. At the reference prices only the 4-person tier clears ₪600/h at base hours, and none clears it with the buffer. A fixed price survives a larger household only if set with the buffer in mind or with stated inclusions and a price per extra.

## 4. Optional annual care retainer (past clients)

Assumption: 16 h a year = 2 visits (2 h travel + 2 h on site each, 8 h), remote/on-call 6 h, admin 2 h; one replacement key a year (₪381 net). A light version of 10 h (1 visit + remote) is shown for contrast. Percent is of the two-person price (₪26,000 today, ₪36,000 mid grid).

| Retainer (gross) | % of ₪26k / ₪36k | Net contribution after key | ₪/h at 16 h | ₪/h at 10 h |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ₪2,600 | 10% / 7% | ₪1,822 | 114 | 182 |
| ₪3,900 | 15% / 11% | ₪2,924 | 183 | 292 |
| ₪5,200 | 20% / 14% | ₪4,026 | 252 | 403 |
| ₪6,500 | 25% / 18% | ₪5,127 | 320 | 513 |
| ₪9,890 | 38% / 27% | ₪8,000 | 500 | n/a |

A 10-20% retainer pays ₪114-252/h, well below any engagement, so it competes for the same 80 h. Twenty retainer clients use 320 h a year (about 27 h/month, a third of capacity). It matches ₪500/h only at about ₪9,900 (38% of today's two-person price), which is a care plan priced as a product, not an add-on. The case for it rests on retention and referrals, not on ₪/h.

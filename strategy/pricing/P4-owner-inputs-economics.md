## What this shows
1. **The main product earns the least per hour.** With the credit, travel and no time saving from the Assessment, the Shield path yields ~₪396 net/h — below the secondary-source senior consultant benchmark of ~₪4,800/day (~₪600/h) in P1.
2. **The credit costs a full Assessment.** Because the Assessment saves no Shield hours, every converted client is 8 unpaid hours (₪3,051 net).
3. **Inner Circle is the best-paying engagement** at 4 days; if it really takes 5–6 days, it falls to ₪530–640/h.
4. **Fixed prices with variable households need explicit fences.** Each tier should state what is included (people, devices, accounts) and how extras are priced, or the fixed price must carry a margin for the variance.
5. **Extra person (P3 proposal ~₪12,000):** ₪10,169 net less keys ₪763. If one extra person adds ~8 h, that is ~₪1,175/h; if a full 16 h plus a visit, ~₪520/h. Either way it beats the Shield path.

## Options for the owner (decision required; none applied)
- A. Hold prices; add fences (included devices/accounts per person; fixed price per extra device/account) to protect the fixed price.
- B. Keep the credit, but limit it (e.g. credit part of the fee) — restores ~₪1,500–3,000 per converted client.
- C. Raise Personal Shield to bring the Shield path to ~₪550–600/h (≈ ₪18,500–20,500); test first with interviews (P2 §5).
- D. Introduce the 2-person price (P3) — fits a typical 2-person household and earns more per hour than a single Shield.

# P8 — Annual Care (16 Oct 2026)

Basis: P7, P7z, P4–P6, BRIEF, V-022…V-030. Skills: offers (value equation, anatomy), pricing (value metric, round premium levels), churn-prevention (touchpoints, renewal moment, rejoin path). Prices VAT-inclusive, unlabelled.

## 1. Purpose and who it's for

**Past clients only**, after the 60-day question line (V-026). Outcome: "my setup stays right, and I have one person to ask." Why people renew:
- **Devices change.** Phones are replaced, updates reset settings, new accounts appear.
- **People change.** A new partner, a child's first phone, a new assistant or lawyer.
- **Situations escalate.** Hearings, settlement talks or an estate dispute bring pressure back months later.
- **Continuity.** He knows the setup; answers come in a day.

## 2. Plan structure

**One plan, priced by the engagement it follows** (value metric: people protected). The client hears one number: theirs.

**Included, for 12 months:**

| Inclusion | Shield (1) | for Two | Inner Circle (≤4) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Question line, same terms as the 60 days (WhatsApp/Signal, one working day, questions only) | yes | yes | yes |
| Scheduled check visit in person: devices, accounts, recovery settings and keys re-checked | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| New or replaced phone or computer set up the Toza way (at the check or one extra visit) | 1 visit | 1 visit | 1 visit, up to 2 devices |
| Replacement key if lost or broken, configured and tested | 1 | 1 | 2 |
| Written update to the dated record after the check | yes | yes | yes |
| First available appointment, ahead of new enquiries | yes | yes | yes |
| Coordination with your lawyer or family office, if authorised | yes | yes | yes |

**Hardware.** Further keys and any replacement router at cost. Replacing a device is covered; adding devices is not.

**Inner Circle:** the included twelve-month review stays; in the first Care term the check visit moves to about month six.

**Not included (scoped separately, first-available scheduling):** new people; a new or second residence; company devices or staff; devices beyond the original allowance; any incident (suspected compromise, active investigation); urgent visits (urgent terms unchanged, V-027). Travel outside Central Israel by arrangement, in conversation (V-029).

**Cap: 8 plans, internal, never stated** (no scarcity claims). At 8 (mix 3/3/2): ≈₪8.2k net/month for 15.6 h. At a 25–40% take rate the cap binds in year two, not year one.

**Terms.**
- Annual, prepaid; the term starts the day the 60-day line ends.
- **Joining:** offered at day 50; open until **six months** after the 60-day line ends. After that, a first step (₪4,000), counted in full toward Care within 30 days. Cover starts from a current setup, not once trouble starts.
- **Renewal:** at the check visit, alongside the written update; invoice 30 days before term end; the line stays open 14 days while the invoice is settled. Renewal at the current published price.
- **Retention:** a note at month six if no question has come in ("any new phone or account?"); the check visit is the value recap. Non-renewal: one question why, no save discount; the six-month rejoin window applies.

## 4. Name

**Keep "Annual Care."** Already the owner's term (V-026), plain about the term, and fits the first step's "senior check-up" frame. Rejected: *Retainer* (hours, legal billing), *Membership* (consumer), *Shield Plus* (upsell), *Concierge* (luxury register), *Continuing Protection* (implies a guarantee).

# Round 1 — Conversion & perception

## 1. First 5 seconds
She must feel "quiet, discreet, one careful person, not a tech firm" and see she can write privately now: headline, lead and the three channels readable on first paint, nothing animating over them.

## 2. CTA plan
- **1440 hero:** "WhatsApp" (solid) + "Signal" + "Call me back" directly under the lead, then "In person · With you present · You control your credentials". First paint, no reveal.
- **1440 sticky:** header right carries the same three, compact, from load to footer. Over the photo: no bar (P7); after the hero it fades (opacity) to white with a hairline. "Message" alone is a second tap: not enough.
- **390:** header = wordmark + "Menu". Fixed bottom bar, three equal buttons (≥ 48 px, safe-area padding), visible from load, never hides. Hero text padded above it.
- **Pinned scene / sticky boxes:** header and bar live outside the pinned container, z-index above every sticky box. No scroll-jacking, no snap.
- **Close:** "Start with a private conversation." + "Your message receives a personal reply. The first conversation is free and commits you to nothing." + the three buttons full size (bar stays on 390).

## 4. Hero
- **H2:** "a careful, ordinary desk": calm, document-like, most legible. Fits **h-hero-wide.jpg**; at 390 crop to hand and phone.
- **Primes-style:** "this could be you, today": identification. Fits **Tamara**. The bottom-left lands on the bright tablecloth.
- **Legibility:** gradient to ~65% black (stronger than Primes); text ≥ 4.5:1 over the brightest pixel behind it; buttons white with ink; no text over face or blurred screen; text block ≤ 560 px; at 390 the stack ends above the bar. Check mirrored (RTL) placement later.

## 5. Tiers
- Order: "Fees" + intro → "Start here" band: "Private Exposure Assessment" "₪4,000" + "Counts in full toward your protection if you continue within 30 days." → "Personal Shield" → "Personal Shield for Two" → "Inner Circle" → notes → "All protection tiers →".
- No "recommended" tier, no running border light: upselling under stress erodes trust. "Start here" is the only emphasis; it lowers the threshold to ₪4,000.
- Prices static, same size as the tier name, lining figures, "₪" as in copy. Three across at 1440, stacked at 390. The "Start here" band may pin (D71) but never covers a price.

## 6. Red flags
Loader or wipe before the buttons; contact only behind "Menu"/"Message"; rolling prices; "recommended" badges; scroll-jacking; situations in a carousel; dots or code-like textures; white on mint; spinning marks; more than one motion move per screen; weak hero gradient; long pins with no text; clinic cream + navy.

## 7. Non-negotiables / concessions
1. Three channels on first paint and one tap away at every scroll position, 1440 and 390, above every pinned or sticky layer.
2. Prices static and readable at once: no odometer, count-up or "recommended" pressure.
3. Hero text readable at first paint; all four situations scannable without swiping.

Concede: which hero wins; pin length on desktop; one still dot field in a gap cell; the cropped wordmark box; one band wipe on a section title.

**3. CTA persistence**

**4. A4 dots**
- **Position (adapted).** Keep them, but not black. Use warm #CFCAC1 squares on stone #F3F1EC with a ≤15% mint glow, in one gap cell of the close, and put no text on them.
- **Reply to conversion ("binary/surveillance").** Black dither does read as data. A warm, low-contrast dither reads as linen weave.

**5. A7 odometer**
- **Position.** Strike it. Prices stay static, roman, with lining figures.

**6. P6 band wipe**

- **Primes hero.** Here it is only a 3 px mint rule wiping under each line. A full mint band behind white text would drop to 1.19:1.

**7. Tiers**
- **Pinning and order.** Pin only the "Fees" box, never a tier, so all three prices stay comparable side by side. Keep conversion's full-width "Start here" band before the tiers, on white with a hairline frame and a 6 px mint mark.
- **"Start here" stays white, not mint.** Mint fills exactly one box ("Or before anything happens…"), as the art director and I both hold.
- **Fills.** Stone / sand / ink, ordered by household size. I accept the art director's offsets (+0.2 / +0.4 vh).

**8. Palette extras**
- **Position.** Hold warm stone #F3F1EC / sand #E4DFD6 over cool #E0E0E0.
- **Reply to the art director (Aspen fidelity).** Contrast is equivalent: ink on sand 11.8 against 12.0 on grey. Cool grey sets the wood hangers, amber window, beige stairwell and Tamara's browns against a clinical tone. Warm stone shares their temperature.

- **Phone.** Stacked, no carousel. I agree with the others.

**10. Motion engine**
- **Position.** No objection to vendored `motion`.
- **One colour condition.** For A8, start at opacity .55, not .3. On white that gives about #868686, roughly 3.6:1, which passes for large text (method line ≥24 px). At .3 it would be about 1.9:1.

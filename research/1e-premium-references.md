# Toza: premium references to replace the "three stages" section

Method: pages read as text only. Layout is inferred from headings, alt text and copy length.

Set aside: BlackCloak shows exactly what to avoid: a five-step "How it works", stock photos, statistics and "Get a Demo". Control Risks and Mishcon are corporate news hubs. Lombard Odier and Brunswick are weaker versions of Pictet.

## The four references

**1. Hakluyt**, hakluytandco.com
- *Instead of a process:* nothing. The home page is about 20 words: navigation, an email address and photos of the Mayfair office. Everything is on About.
- *Density:* close to zero on the home page. About has about 300 words in long, measured sentences.
- *People:* nobody is named on the home page. Heads of practice appear on About only.
- *Rigour without diagrams:* selectivity ("we take on work only once we are confident we can provide value"), scale stated as fact, and "Trusted for 30 years".
- *Call to action:* only an email address.

**2. Schillings** (reputation and privacy for very wealthy clients), schillingspartners.com
- *Instead of a process:* a two-word headline, "High stakes, handled.", then six outcome pairs set as large type: "Personal privacy, assured." "Unfair accusations, rebutted." "Hostile action, countered." "Complex situations, navigated."
- *Density:* about 80 words. Each line is a fragment of two to six words, plus one sentence of purpose.
- *People:* nobody is named on the home page. People have their own page.
- *Rigour without diagrams:* five disciplines (Intelligence, Law, Communications, Security, Diplomacy), each with one line of five or six words.
- *Call to action:* "24/7 Immediate Response" with a phone number, plus a plain "Contact".

**3. Pictet**, pictet.com
- *Instead of a process:* a single question as the hero: "How do you like your calm? Before, during or after the storm?" Then one sentence on why clients come ("attention, time, and expertise").
- *Density:* the hero is about 45 words. The page is about 1,000, mostly articles lower down.
- *People:* partners appear by name only in press interviews, never as the brand.
- *Rigour without diagrams:* "Since 1805", plus four bare numbers with small labels (CHF 810bn, 21.9% capital ratio, 172% liquidity, 5,600+ staff). Principles take three sentences.
- *Call to action:* quiet choices ("Individuals and Families") and a contacts page. Nothing is sold.

**4. Gavin de Becker & Associates**, gdba.com
- *Instead of a process:* credentials. Protectors in sequence, each with one sentence of background ("recruited from the FBI…", "protected two U.S. Presidents").
- *Density:* about 570 words of short biography sentences.
- *People:* fully named. Toza should not copy that, but the sentence format works without names.
- *Rigour without diagrams:* a named proprietary method (MOSAIC) and scale stated as fact: "more than 80 of the world's most prominent families".
- *Call to action:* "Contact Us To Learn More" after each block, with no urgency.

## Transferable patterns for Toza

1. **Absence as a signal.** One statement and one invitation. Everything else is one click away. (Hakluyt)
2. **Outcome pairs instead of steps.** Show the end state as two-word lines in large serif type, for example "Private matters, kept private." (Schillings)
3. **A single question as the hero.** Ask the reader something that puts them in the calm moment before a crisis. (Pictet)
4. **Selectivity as proof of rigour.** Say who Toza declines and why, and that work starts only when it can help. (Hakluyt)
5. **Bare facts set as type.** Three or four numbers or dates with small labels, no icons. (Pictet, GDBA)
6. **Background without names.** One sentence on where the expertise comes from, with the founder named only on About. (GDBA, Hakluyt About)
7. **A calm direct line.** One quiet channel, such as a phone number or "Write to us", instead of a booking funnel. (Schillings, Hakluyt)

## Three section concepts for Toza

**A. "Kept private" (outcome ledger)**
*Shows:* five or six lines in large Frank Ruhl type, each a two-word pair in ink on ivory, with brass hairlines between them. For example: "Devices, cleaned." "Accounts, closed to others." "Old exposures, removed." "Household, aligned." "Leverage, removed."
*Why premium:* it reads like an engraved statement and shows confidence in the result, not the steps.
*Words:* about 25.

**B. "The question" (single statement)**
*Shows:* one full-width question in large serif type with plenty of space around it, such as "If someone wanted leverage over you, what would they find?" Under it is one line: "We find it first, in person, and close it." Then a quiet text link: "Begin with a private review."
*Why premium:* one idea given the whole screen. It replaces the process with the reason to act.
*Words:* about 30.

**C. "Terms of engagement" (principles and facts)**
*Shows:* two columns. On the left are four short principles about how Toza works (for example "In person, never remote." "Nothing leaves your home." "We decline more than we accept." "One adviser, start to finish"). On the right are three bare facts in brass numerals with small labels (for example a starting fee, the client limit per year, years of practice). This replaces the stages on How it works.
*Why premium:* private-bank vocabulary. Method is shown through restraint and selectivity, without a diagram.
*Words:* about 50.

## Lead's note (18 Oct): examples corrected to Toza's facts
Some example lines above make claims Toza cannot support ("Nothing leaves your home", "We decline more than we accept", "Leverage, removed", "One adviser, start to finish") or break the Toza voice (V-045). Fact-based versions:
- **A (outcome lines):** "Devices, rebuilt around your risks." · "Recovery, back in your hands." · "Access, only where you choose." · "Every change, tested with you." · "A private record, dated."
- **B (the question):** "If someone wanted leverage over you, what would they find?" · "Toza finds it first, in person, and closes what can be closed." · link: "Begin with the first step."
- **C (terms of engagement):** principles "In person, at your home or office." · "Your passwords stay yours." · "Nothing changes without your approval." · "One fixed price, agreed in writing."; facts in brass numerals: ₪4,000 first step · 30 days to count it in full · 60 days of answered questions. No invented client limits or years.

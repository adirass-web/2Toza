# Toza: Search demand, keyword map and ad feasibility (Israel)

Prepared 2026-10-15. Method note: WebSearch returned result titles and URLs only, with no volume or CPC data for Hebrew terms. No Israeli keyword-tool figures could be obtained. **All Hebrew volumes and CPCs are "unknown, needs Keyword Planner"**. Planning ranges below are labelled assumptions, not findings. SERP observations are based on result titles/domains surfaced by search, not a full manual SERP audit (personalised/mobile SERPs will differ).

## 1. Keyword map

Hebrew spellings to cover: פריצה/נפרץ/פרצו, וואטסאפ/ווצאפ/ואטסאפ/וואטספ, אייפון/איפון, סלולרי/טלפון/נייד, האזנה/ריגול/מעקב, גירושין/גירושים, אבטחת מידע/סייבר/אבטחת סייבר. Common slang: "חדרו לי", "עוקבים אחרי", "מרגלים אחרי". Hebrew keyword tools often merge spelling variants, but Google Ads exact/phrase matching does not always, so add the variants.

## 3. Competitive SERP sense

Searches run: "האם הטלפון שלי נפרץ", "מישהו קורא לי את הוואטסאפ", "יועץ אבטחת מידע פרטי...", "הגנה על פרטיות בגירושין...", "חוקר פרטי גילוי האזנות...", "הסרת תוכנת ריגול...". Titles/domains returned:

| Query theme | Who appears (organic) | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| Phone hacked check | ESET IL, NordVPN HE, Kipa, Ice, BHOL, Calcalist | Consumer antivirus/VPN and news own informational queries. Toza cannot out-rank them there; don't spend paid budget |
| Someone reading WhatsApp | Mako Nexter, Ice, Gadgety, Samsung community | Same: how-to media. A Toza guide could still rank for long tail |
| Private security consultant / phone protection | Mako, Ice, Ynet, Kipa (National Cyber Directorate warnings), Calcalist, FTC consumer tips | No obvious dedicated "private-client digital security consultant" competitor surfaced. This is a positioning gap, but also a sign that vocabulary/demand is undeveloped |
| Spousal surveillance and divorce | Ice (family-law blog on wiretapping), Maariv, Mako, Hon, Calcalist | Legal/media framing; lawyers explain the law. Toza should link to but not imitate legal advice |
| Investigator/spyware | Ynet on spy apps and a police arrest of investigators for illegal wiretapping, Walla, Maariv on Pegasus affair, d.co.il directory price lists, cyber course pages | This SERP is investigators and trade directories. Toza should avoid it entirely. Ad copy should stay away from "investigator"/"spyware" wording |

Implications: (1) paid ads should be on commercial/service phrasing ("יועץ", "הקשחה", "אבטחה אישית") not "check/detect"; (2) ad copy must state in-person, one expert, client's own devices, no investigation, no legal advice, so wrong-intent clickers self-filter; (3) landing page needs a clear "what we do not do" block; (4) price signal (from ₪3,600) in copy or on page reduces low-budget clicks. Verify SERPs live with an incognito Hebrew search and the Ads Auction Insights after launch.

## 4. Feasibility: ₪10,000 over 3 months

Budget about ₪3,300/month, about ₪110/day. Keep ₪500–800 flexible for week-2 corrections.

**Ad groups (Search only, no Display/PMax expansion, no search partners at start):**
1. **Private security consultant (Hebrew, generic):** phrase and exact: "יועץ אבטחת מידע פרטי", "יועץ סייבר פרטי", "הקשחת טלפון", "אבטחה דיגיטלית אישית", "אבטחת סייבר למנהלים". Highest scope fit.
2. **Situation: divorce/separation/dispute (Hebrew):** phrase/exact: "הגנה על פרטיות בגירושין", "אבטחה דיגיטלית בגירושין", "אבטחת חשבונות אחרי פרידה", "ניתוק גישה לחשבונות בן זוג". Sensitive and thin volume; very tight copy.
3. **English/expat:** phrase/exact: "personal cybersecurity consultant Israel", "protect my phone from being hacked Tel Aviv", "privacy hardening". Small volume; run only if budget allows (about 15%).

Allocation: about 50% / 35% / 15%. Skip "phone hacked" generic phrases at first. Test later at low budget only if ad group 1 shows good lead quality.

**Match types:** exact and phrase only; no broad match without a conversion history (broad plus Smart Bidding will drift into investigator and spyware queries). Start Maximise Clicks with a CPC cap (about ₪15), move to Maximise Conversions only after about 15–30 conversions (unlikely at this budget). Count only form/WhatsApp/call-intake events as conversions.

**Geo:** Central Israel first (Tel Aviv district, Gush Dan, Herzliya/Ra'anana/Kfar Saba, Sharon, Modiin), "presence" targeting only (not "interest in"). Add Jerusalem and Haifa area after 4–6 weeks if lead quality holds. Schedule: all hours initially; review hour-of-day data after 4 weeks, as late-night distress searches may be low quality or high value.

**Negative keywords (account-level list, add from day 1):** חוקר, חוקרים, חוקר פרטי, בגידה, בוגד, לעקוב, מעקב, ריגול, תוכנת ריגול, אפליקציית ריגול, האזנה, האזנות סתר, פורנזיקה, ראיות, שחזור, מחיקה, חינם, אפליקציה, אנטי וירוס, VPN, קורס, לימודים, משרה, עבודה, מחיר (review), עורך דין, תביעה, פגסוס, bug, spy, spyware, stalkerware, investigator, detective, forensic, free, jobs, course, track, monitor, hack (do not add "hack" as negative if you want "protect from hack" terms; use phrase negatives like "how to hack", "לפרוץ"). Review the search terms report daily for the first 2 weeks.

**Expected clicks (assumptions: ₪10,000 spend, blended CPC ₪6–14):** roughly 700–1,650 clicks over 3 months. If real CPC is ₪15–20 (competitive/legal-adjacent), 500–670 clicks. Risk: impression volume cap. Exact-match niche terms may simply not deliver ₪110/day; underspend is plausible, and in that case widen phrase variants rather than broad match.

**Conversations (assumptions, my own):** landing-page inquiry rate 1.5–4% for a high-ticket, sensitive service. That gives about 10–65 inquiries. Qualified and in-scope (right budget, right request) 20–35% means about 3–20 qualified conversations. Closing 20–30% of those means roughly 1–5 paid Assessments (₪3,600), with a small chance of an upsell. Revenue break-even on ₪10,000 needs about 3 assessments or 1 Personal Shield (₪14,000). Treat the campaign as a lead-quality test, not a volume play. Referral and direct outreach will probably outperform it.

### Policy and ethical risks
- **Google Ads:** since August 2020 Google bans ads for stalkerware and covert tracking tools (spyware, secret GPS trackers, hidden cameras marketed for spying), with an exception for private-investigation services and parental monitoring ([BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/google-will-ban-ads-for-stalkerware-starting-august-11)). Toza is a defensive service, so it should be allowed, but ad copy and keywords touching "spyware", "tracking" or "monitor" risk disapproval or manual review. Keep wording about protecting the client's own devices. Check the current policy page before launch (the policy has been revised since 2020).
- **Sensitive situations:** Google's personalised-advertising rules restrict targeting by sensitive categories; do not use remarketing/audience lists or copy implying you know the viewer's marital or legal status ("Is your spouse spying on you?"). Avoid fear-based or accusatory copy.
- **Ethics:** do not target people who may be seeking to surveil others, do not imply detecting a partner's snooping is guaranteed, and do not promise outcomes in a legal dispute. Do not imply legal advice or evidence value. Add a line that Toza is not an investigation, forensic or legal service.
- **Privacy/tracking:** under the Israeli Privacy Protection Law, keep form data minimal, and do not log sensitive situation details in ad platforms. Prefer a neutral intake form. Avoid heavy session recorders on the intake page.
- **Hebrew ads review:** Hebrew-language disapprovals can be slow to appeal; submit ads early (1–2 weeks pre-launch).

## 5. SEO notes (organic, Hebrew)

Realistic ranking prospects for a new domain:
- **Likely achievable (long tail, low competition):** pages on "הקשחת טלפון לאנשים פרטיים", "יועץ אבטחת מידע פרטי", "אבטחה דיגיטלית בגירושין", "אבטחת חשבונות אחרי פרידה". Few dedicated competitors surfaced, so a focused page with real expertise (E-E-A-T: Dr. Tabansky's credentials, plain Hebrew, FAQ schema) can place.
- **Hard (dominated by Ynet, Mako, Kipa, ESET, NordVPN):** "הטלפון שלי נפרץ", "מישהו קורא את הוואטסאפ שלי". Only target with a differentiated guide (what to do and what not to do; why scanning is not enough; when to get a human expert), written as a "first 24 hours" checklist, not a scan tutorial.
- **Avoid:** investigator/spyware terms.

Suggested pages: (1) one situation page each: divorce/separation, business/founder dispute, inheritance conflict, "they know something", executive/family protection; (2) pricing and process page (what is included, what is not); (3) three guides: WhatsApp linked-devices, account recovery and 2FA/security keys, "my phone may be accessed: first steps"; (4) English mirror for the top 2 pages; (5) a short "boundaries" page: not investigation, forensics or legal advice (supports trust and filters leads).

**Content gaps seen:** no result in the sampled SERPs was a boutique personal-security service with transparent scope and pricing; divorce-related content is framed by lawyers (legality) with no practical hardening checklist; AI-answer visibility: concise, citable definitions and step lists help (see marketing-skills:ai-seo approach); Hebrew-language "security key setup" guides appear thin (unverified; check).

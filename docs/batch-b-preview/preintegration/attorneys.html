When a referral may help

  • Someone appears to know private information the client did not knowingly share.
  • The client is unsure which devices, recovery routes or shared services still connect them to a former partner, colleague or relative.
  • Password changes have not clarified who can regain access or see shared information.
  • Personal accounts or devices overlap with a business or family dispute.
  • The client needs to understand the consequences of changes before authorizing them.

These concerns can justify a review. They do not establish unauthorized access or wrongdoing.

Work starts with the client's authority

A referral does not authorize access to devices, accounts or findings. The client must authorize the work and have lawful authority over the environment being reviewed. They remain present, control their credentials and approve material changes.

Toza does not keep credentials for ongoing use, remotely control or monitor devices, or install security software on them. Coordination with counsel requires the client's permission; the referring attorney is not automatically a recipient of personal information or findings.

Coordinate consequential changes

Changing recovery details, ending shared access or separating services can interrupt access or create notifications. Toza explains the relevant effects before material changes are approved. With the client's permission, timing can be coordinated with counsel. Legal strategy remains with the attorney; the client authorizes technical changes.

A practical summary, with clear limits

With the client's explicit authorization, an attorney-friendly summary can describe the work and remaining issues. The client decides what may be disclosed. A summary or dated record does not establish what another person did.

Toza does not investigate other people, reconstruct events forensically, preserve a chain of custody, prepare court evidence or provide legal conclusions. The work and its records are not a forensic report or evidence document and do not guarantee that every exposure is found or removed.

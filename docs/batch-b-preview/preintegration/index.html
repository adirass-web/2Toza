Personal digital security and privacy

Private information can be used against you.

A private conversation, financial information or plans you have not shared publicly can be used to pressure you, cost you money or damage your reputation. The access may come from ordinary arrangements you have never had reason to question.

Dr. Lior Tabansky works with you, in person, to assess relevant exposure and carry out agreed technical changes. You can choose a more secure, more private digital life before you notice a problem. You do not have to work out where to look first.

Close unwanted access. Build stronger security habits.

Personal Shield and Inner Circle Shield harden the agreed devices, digital identities, accounts, apps and access to data around your circumstances. The work can include closing unwanted access, strengthening sign-in and recovery, and changing digital identity and sharing arrangements where needed.

Dr. Tabansky carries out the agreed changes, tests material changes and teaches you how to use the resulting setup. Practical habits are part of the work: how you respond to approaches, share information and grant access, as well as how you sign in and recover accounts.

Each full engagement follows the Assessment and has a separately agreed scope, price and purchase. Toza does not monitor your devices or install security software on them.

Everyday connections can reveal more than you expect.

Someone who knows you may also know personal details that help them seek account access or gain your trust. Shared services and permissions can keep private information accessible. Information from one part of your life can help someone reach another.

An independent copy of a private document can remain on an old computer or in a backup after you change the account it came from.

Help with an account or sharing with someone you trusted can leave access in place after life changes. That does not establish misuse. What matters is what remains connected and what you want to change.

The situation changes the work.

Dr. Tabansky considers what needs to stay private, how it could be reached and what changing access would affect. This is personal threat modelling: setting priorities around your circumstances and the services you still need, so you can decide who should retain access.

Your confidence matters. Your approval directs the work.

Your situation and the information you share are handled confidentially. You stay present, approve material changes and control your credentials. Toza does not keep them for ongoing use or remotely control your devices.

Service boundaries and privacy details

Work stays within your authorized environment and agreed scope. We do not promise to find or remove every exposure. We do not investigate another person, reconstruct events forensically, collect legal evidence or give legal advice.

When private information surfaces

Someone seems to know something private. What now?

Someone quotes a private conversation, knows about a plan you have not shared with them, or hints at information they could use against you. You may not know what they actually have or how it reached them.

Start with what can be checked

Relevant exposure may involve several connected devices, identities or services, rather than the account you first suspect. Dr. Tabansky examines those relationships and explains what they can reveal or allow. Location settings, public information and online trails may also matter within the agreed review of your own environment.

The findings can identify access worth changing now, even when they cannot explain the original concern.

Understand the effects before making isolated changes

Changing a password may leave another session or recovery route open. Deleting an account can also affect services you still need. We explain those connections and agree the sequence of changes with you.

Some changes can be noticed, such as a signed-in device being disconnected. We do not promise invisibility. The aim is informed action within the access you are authorized to manage.

Full services, prices and credit conditions

What the findings can—and cannot—tell you

We review what is disclosed, observable or otherwise lawfully accessible within your authorized environment and agreed scope. We do not prove how information reached someone else, identify an actor, investigate an investigator, reconstruct events forensically or collect court evidence. Recovering copies of information already shared is outside this service. We do not guarantee that every exposure will be found or removed.

An attorney or adviser can receive a summary only with your authorization. It remains a practical account of the work, not legal advice or a forensic report.

You remain present, approve material changes and control your credentials. Toza does not keep them for ongoing use, remotely control your devices, monitor them or install security software on them.

Privacy and service boundaries

You can start without an explanation.

Send a short, neutral WhatsApp or Signal message. Dr. Tabansky replies personally as soon as possible. The first conversation checks fit and urgency; it includes no device inspection, mapping, diagnosis or technical work. Contacting Toza does not commit you to purchase.

Suggested message: “I'd like a private conversation. Please let me know the next step.”

Do not send passwords, recovery codes, screenshots, legal documents, intimate material or details, or detailed allegations in the first message. Contact through WhatsApp or Signal is not guaranteed to be invisible on your device or account.

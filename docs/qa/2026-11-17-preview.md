# Preview QA and remediation — 17 November 2026

Baseline: Cloudflare preview for main commit `269b7197c3d0276687c55741283fbbfaa83c6f3f`. SquirrelScan 0.0.182 ran offline in quick HTTP mode across 13 pages. Its score is not a production score: the preview deliberately disallows indexing and the crawl did not render pages, check external links, or validate TLS. Browser traffic traversed the environment proxy.

The shared home link's accessible name now includes its visible tagline; the wordmark has intrinsic dimensions; a favicon is linked and available at `/favicon.ico`; and About, Exposure and How it works have descriptions based on their existing page content. Preview noindex remains unchanged.

A hosted Playwright sweep of 11 routes at 320, 390, 768 and 1440 pixels found no horizontal overflow, missing image alt attributes, broken loaded images or uncaught page errors in the tested conditions. Hosted axe WCAG 2 A/AA and 2.1 A/AA checks of 13 routes at phone width returned no automated violations. These checks do not replace manual assistive technology testing. Pricing's first disclosure opened in browser testing.

The FAQ and Conditions table reports are false positives: their first column uses scoped row headers. The reported orphan page is linked from Attorneys. Scanner timing and loading warnings need representative network and browser measurements before changes. Preview headers already include frame, MIME, referrer and permissions protections; a CSP requires compatibility testing, and HSTS needs a decision for the eventual production domain and subdomains. Canonicals, sitemap, indexing and structured data should be revisited against the final domain and verified business information. TLS, external contact links, full keyboard and screen-reader review remain outstanding.

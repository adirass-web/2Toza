## Build the paid destinations

| Section | Website | Paid search |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening | Lead with the stakes specific to the situation and how details across accounts can expose them. | Name the service and situation immediately. Match the search and ad wording, with the contact action in the first screen. |
| Protection | Explain what matters, what could expose it and what the tailored work changes. | Show one exposure path and one example within the client’s authority. Explain that Toza designs, carries out and tests the agreed work in person. |
| Getting started | Explain scope, charges and follow-up, with relevant adviser or FAQ links. | Explain the free conversation, ₪4,000 assessment, separately agreed protection and credit terms in a compact block. Keep Pricing within reach. |
| Contact | Repeat the same action and approved first-message guidance. | Use the same contact components. Keep the route crawlable for review, noindex and outside the sitemap. |

## Prepare and run the pilot

- [ ] Confirm service area, language, intake owner and response capacity. Confirm the service examples and claims with the service owner, and give a paid-search reviewer the actual ads, keywords, pages and measurement plan.
- [ ] Validate demand and cost in Keyword Planner. Draft two responsive search concepts per group, one about retained access and one about protecting what a decision could reveal. Review exact and phrase match and negatives for surveillance, investigation and legal-service intent.
- [ ] Prepare aggregate first-party counts and define a qualified inquiry. Separate WhatsApp and Signal opens from conversations and bookings. Keep personal details out of campaign events and avoid remarketing and enhanced conversions for this pilot.
- [ ] Prepare the campaigns paused for owner review, with the proposed budget, spend ceiling and cost per qualified inquiry ceiling. Enable only after approval and destination verification. Review search terms daily and assess qualified inquiries and booked assessments after the two-week pilot.

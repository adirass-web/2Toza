# Empty enquiry baseline — 13 October 2026

This implements section 11 of the approved execution plan. The CSV is an empty business template, not observed results. No tracking code, contact event, CRM or session recording is added.

Copy [the template](enquiry-baseline-template.csv) to an appropriate private business system before recording actual information. Keep real client records out of the public repository. Use weekly aggregate counts and nonsensitive category labels only; no names, message contents, identifiers, intimate facts, credentials or allegations.

| Field | Definition |
|---|---|
| week_start | Start date of one consistent seven-day reporting period, using Asia/Jerusalem. |
| actual_enquiries | New people who actually contact Toza about possible service. Count each person once at their initial enquiry; do not recount follow-ups in later weeks. A button click is not an enquiry. |
| qualified_enquiries | Enquiries for which the direct conversation establishes a plausible service fit within the client's authority and Toza's scope. |
| assessments_booked | Initial paid visits scheduled after explicit acceptance and receipt of payment; do not count an expression of interest as a booking. |
| assessments_completed | Initial paid visits completed in that reporting period. |
| full_engagements_accepted | Separately scoped full engagements explicitly accepted in that period; this is not a claim of completed work or paid revenue. |
| volunteered_source | Nonsensitive aggregate tallies of sources people volunteered, or mixed/unknown. Use unknown when not supplied; do not infer tracking or attribution. |
| nonsensitive_objection_category | Brief aggregate categories such as unclear service, timing, price, authority/scope or unknown. No personal narrative. |

Use one row per week. Do not duplicate the week's totals in extra rows for different source or objection categories. These are separate stage totals, not automatically a same-week funnel: someone may enquire one week and book or complete later. Small volumes and unknown traffic do not justify a conversion forecast, significance claim or arbitrary test winner. Use repeated questions and actual stage progress to identify the next useful change.

The template is available without making customer interviews, analytics installation or future enquiry volume prerequisites for the current design review.

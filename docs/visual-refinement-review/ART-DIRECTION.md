### 1. Make the Business purchase-stage boundary visible — recommended next prototype

**Observed fact:** `/en/business-dispute.html`, 1440×900, `local-business-dispute-desktop-offer.png`: one pale-green region contains the Assessment title, ₪3,600 fee, Assessment scope and independent-summary paragraphs, and the separate Personal Shield paragraph. At 390×844, `local-business-dispute-mobile-offer.png`, the Assessment explanation fills most of the viewport and the Personal Shield paragraph begins with the same body treatment below it. Desktop/mobile full images corroborate the continuous region.

**Inference / commercial reason:** A fast scan can visually attach the later hardening description to the Assessment amount. The exact copy explicitly says its scope and price require a separate decision, so this is not a factual contradiction or evidence of an actual client misunderstanding. It is a stronger layout opportunity than moving Why's already-linked profile. Priority is medium with high confidence in the visual observation.

**Exact content boundary:** Within `Put the technical questions in experienced hands`, preserve the existing Assessment identity/duration, fee, complete paragraph beginning “The paid visit includes hands-on inspection”, complete paragraph beginning “You receive a summary”, complete paragraph beginning “Personal Shield hardens”, and both existing links in their original order. Source: `revisions/en-06/01-BUSINESS-CANDIDATE.md`. Do not introduce a new heading, stage number, caption, service promise or repeated fee.

**Recommendation:** Visually close the Assessment group after its independent-summary paragraph, then place the entire existing Personal Shield paragraph in a distinct adjacent group using a clear spacing interval and thin rule or restrained paper surface. Its opening existing service-name words can receive modest weight if needed. Keep all of the paragraph, especially the separate scope/price decision, together and visible. Retain the two original links after it. Use one vertical sequence on desktop and mobile; a side-by-side layout could imply two equivalent purchasable options and is unnecessary.

**Tradeoff:** A surface inside a surface could become fussy. Prefer the least treatment that makes the purchase boundary apparent. Do not create another general card style or make full hardening seem compulsory. This is the second production component in the bounded pilot, not a whole situation-page redesign.
### 2. Give all four Home situation entries comparable visual recognition — recommended next prototype

**Exact content boundary:** Use the existing `When private information matters differently` heading; its complete proactive paragraph beginning “You do not need a dispute or known incident”; the complete `Separation or divorce` h3 plus its paragraph and existing link; the complete `Business or founder dispute` h3 plus its paragraph and existing link; the complete paragraph beginning the `Private information has surfaced` link; and the complete paragraph beginning the `Inheritance conflict` link. ## How the fitted-panel identity can enter composition

The approved mark consists of two solid fitted panels with a narrow stepped join. Keep those SVG masters untouched. Translate the principle into a content relationship, not a repeated logo-shaped motif:

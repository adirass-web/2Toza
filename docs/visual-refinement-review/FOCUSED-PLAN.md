## Ranked remaining opportunities

1. **Business: distinguish the two purchases visually.** The Assessment price, inspection/first-aid scope and independent summary currently share a continuous shaded band with the separate Personal Shield paragraph. The words distinguish the services correctly, but a skim reader must do more work to recognize the boundary. This is the strongest remaining layout opportunity. Expected benefit: easier association of the Assessment fee with its own scope and clearer recognition of separately agreed full hardening. That benefit remains a heuristic hypothesis.
2. **Home: make the supporting situation links easier to scan.** Separation and Business have existing primary headings/rules; They Know Something and Inheritance are supporting linked paragraphs. Give the latter two restrained, aligned rows while retaining that hierarchy. This is optional: omit it if rendered review shows merely more chrome or height. Expected benefit: easier discovery of a relevant supporting destination without making every visitor choose a dispute.

No third component is required. Keep Why's portrait and professional-profile shortcut, Pricing's early fee and comparison, the grouped FAQ and Process stages. Defer moving the expert-delivery paragraph above situation-page actions: doing so can push those actions down on mobile. The other situation pages already have distinct full-service headings; Business's treatment is not a blanket rollout.

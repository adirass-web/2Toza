# Accessibility statement — owner-approved wording

The owner approved the proposed English wording and its addition to the site. Verify its statements against the completed site and actual service before publication. It is not a determination of legal compliance.

## Proposed English text

**Accessibility**

Toza aims to make this website usable by people with disabilities. The website includes text alternatives for informative images, keyboard navigation and a link to skip to the main content. You can enlarge the text using your browser's zoom controls.

If you encounter an accessibility problem or need information in another format, please contact Toza through [WhatsApp](https://wa.me/972533366276) or [Signal](https://signal.me/#p/+972533366276). Please identify the page and describe the difficulty so we can understand your request. Please do not include sensitive personal information in your first message.

Toza provides its service at locations selected by clients. Please contact Toza to discuss accessibility requirements for your appointment.
